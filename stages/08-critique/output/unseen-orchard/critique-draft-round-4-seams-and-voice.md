## Findings, most important first

### 1. The climax explains the voice, and one pronoun turns the voice into the Witness's

- **Target:** `draft-v6-part3.md`, ch. 10, from "And then, quite clearly" to "It did not help." (lines 195 to 201): "The word he had always said. She knew he had not said it. It was in her own head, in his voice, and it did not say where. / Then it was there, as it had been in the field: the room opened from a side. ... All of them in view together, and none of them hiding any other. It did not choose. It did not help."
- **What is wrong:** four sentences explain where the voice came from, and the next paragraph begins "Then it was there". The same pronoun then names first the voice and then the presence, so a reader can take the Witness as the one who said "Go".
- **Grounds:** `world-v2.md` §10, "Rules kept": the Witness is "never heard", and the voice "must stay ambiguous: a reader can take it as grief and stress, which keeps it 'almost supernatural'". "It was in her own head" settles the voice one way; "Then it was there" tips it the other. The writer's own cut was from "She wanted, stupidly, to ask him what to do." straight to "'Keep us here,' she said." The decision was shown, not explained (dialogue skill, `flaws-and-fixes.md` §4, "On the nose", applied to narration). "None of them hiding any other" is the third use of one image (finding 10), and "It did not choose. It did not help." defines the presence by what it is not (finding 11).
- **Fix** (64 words for 113):

  > And then, quite clearly, as if he were standing behind her, she heard him say it.
  >
  > *Go.*
  >
  > It did not say where.
  >
  > For a moment she had all of them in view at once: Tomas at the outstation, the woman at the hatch, the man with the patch gone dark on his arm, the tug crews burning fuel on the far side of everything.
  >
  > “Keep us here,” she said.

  *Cost:* the stated link back to the field (a reader must connect "in view at once" with ch. 7 alone), and the statement that the presence did not help. The voice, Tomas as the authority it borrows (the Jaynes idea), its two readings and the list all stay.
- **Standing:** bears. The fix keeps the moment loud enough to count as "announced" and restores the plan's own rule.
- **Where it came from:** in part an earlier stage's output (`world-v2.md` §10 has "She knows he did not say it"); "It was in her own head" and "Then it was there" come from this output (rounds 4 and 5).

### 2. The peak of chapter 7 is written in a voice that is not the writer's

- **Target:** `draft-v6-part2.md`, ch. 7, from "She saw herself." to "It said nothing." (lines 229 to 235).
- **What is wrong:** the book's longest abstract passage uses a general "you" ("Not as a mirror shows you"), stacked figures ("as close as breath", "a want that had no floor"), stated feeling ("That was the worst of it") and definition by negation ("It was not unkind. It was not anything."). The writer does none of these.
- **Grounds:** the counts above (general "you" 0 and 2; figures about 7 and about 12). The writer carries terror in the body and in objects: "Nessa felt her mouth go dry"; "Inside the lock she could not make her hands release the rail." Dialogue skill, `flaws-and-fixes.md` §3, "The writer on show"; §2, "Nobody sees themselves that clearly" ("the knot under her breastbone that had been there since the assay room" ties the feeling to its cause for the reader). At 112 words it is the longest paragraph in the middle; the writer's longest there is 76.
- **Fix** (124 words for 174; every object and the event kept):

  > She saw herself. All of her, from the side: the seams of the suit and what was under them, the folded harness, the pocket with the pencil in it, the meal she had eaten, the knot under her breastbone. Nothing was in front of anything else.
  >
  > The looking did not come from her. It came from beside her, from the direction she could not hold in mind, close and in no hurry. It saw the pencil and the knot with the same attention. It did not reach in.
  >
  > She wanted it to. She wanted it to put out a hand and move one thing. It went on looking.
  >
  > She listened, as if it might tell her where to put her hands. It said nothing.

  "From the direction she could not hold in mind" is the writer's own phrase from ch. 3. *Cost:* "a want that had no floor" and "That was the worst of it" go, and with them the loudest statement of the pull towards the presence (Otto's attraction in `world-v2.md` §0); "She wanted it to" still carries it, flatly. "Not as a mirror shows you" also goes; its mirror points at Linet's reversal, and "Not as a mirror would." keeps that without the "you".
- **Standing:** bears. The event, its place and the want stay; only the register changes.
- **Where it came from:** this output (round 1). There is also a sign for the workshop: `brief-v1.md` sets no rule that added prose should match the writer's voice, so no stage was asked to check it. I did not read stage 09's contract, so whether it asks is *not checked*.

### 4. "Go" planted in chapter 1 uses up the writer's reveal in chapter 12
