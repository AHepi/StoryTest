In ch. 7 the frame holds Nessa during the obstructed crossing (part2 l.225), she repins in open space outside Wren (l.237-241), and she later opens the rig record (l.279), so she can know the minute. ## New problems

1. **"Whose consent" now answers nothing (ch. 6).** Draft 1 had the adjudicator's line "A person under a continuing service claim cannot make that commitment without consent." It was cut in draft 2 and not restored, so Dema's restored "Whose consent did she need to be born?" (part2 l.123) has no "consent" on the page to answer. **Fix:** after "Sava witnessed it." (l.101), give the adjudicator: "A person under a continuing service claim cannot make that commitment without consent." About 13 words. **Standing:** bears.
2. **"Tomas on the paused screen" contradicts ch. 10's own line.** Part3 l.157: "Nessa stopped the playback. The word remained in the room after his face had gone." Then l.195 has him still on a paused screen. (Draft 2's "stopped frame" had the same clash, less plainly.) **Fix:** "Tomas at the outstation;" or "Tomas, twenty kilometres off;". **Standing:** bears.
3. **"Frame" in two senses in one sentence (ch. 7).** The DC4 clause puts "before frames checked for mass" (the crossing rig) next to "into the hull's own frame" (the structure), the very slip DC10(a) fixed elsewhere. **Fix:** "into rock and into the hull's own ribs". **Standing:** bears.
4. **"On the evening of the attack" (ch. 9, part3 l.49).** Ch. 7 gives no time of day; it opens on a meal being packed away. Nothing contradicts it, but nothing supports it. **Fix:** "on the day of the attack on Nearbank". **Standing:** does not bear yet. If the writer's timeline puts the attack in the evening, keep it.
5. **A comma run in ch. 5 (part2 l.25).** "He offered to pay for everything, the repairs, the forward operation, the worst of the war debts, if his branch..." reads as a list of five things. **Fix:** "He offered to pay for the repairs, the forward operation and the worst of the war debts, if his branch could name the expedition's commander." **Standing:** bears.
6. **The ch. 3 side-place exchange (part1 l.325-329).** Linet is "finishing an argument" in the sentence before she asks the child, and the whistle sentence now sits in the child's speech paragraph, so it reads as the child's beat. **Fix:** put "The distant whistle had found a fourth note." in a paragraph of its own; and either give the question to Nessa, who is the one looking at the place, or write "Linet broke off to ask the child". **Standing:** the paragraph break bears; who asks is held if the writer wants Linet, a crosser, to be seen not knowing this custom.
7. **"Knocked" and "the knock" three sentences apart (ch. 6, part2 l.147).** Draft 1 kept them in separate paragraphs; the merge brings them together. **Fix:** "By evening the hammering in the pipes had stopped." **Standing:** bears.
8. **"Empty" chair twice in 14 lines (ch. 8, part2 l.283 and l.297).** **Fix:** "Nessa pulled the chair back." **Standing:** bears.
9. **The drift entry is pressed a little (ch. 9, part3 l.49).** "She had not been in the chair. She had not been on the ship." spells out the oddity in two flat sentences, leaning towards the proof SL3 warned against. **Fix:** keep only "She had not been on the ship." **Standing:** held if the writer wants the reading quieter.
