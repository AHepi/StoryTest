# Stage 07: dialogue

**Its one job:** write the talk into the action drafts, so that each line does what its beat needs, in a voice only that character has.

The rules every stage shares are in `stages/how-stages-work.md`, and are not repeated here.

## Process

1. **Read the project log, and the draft, cast, world and plot** for the scenes being written, and record any edits the writer made to them since they were handed over (`stages/how-stages-work.md`, section 8).
2. **Ask the questions** before writing, from each character's side, for the scenes the story turns on (reference, section 2).
3. **Write the three passes, then the voices check** (reference, section 1), keeping to how much each group leaves unsaid (section 3).
4. **Ask again halfway and at the end,** and change the scene where an answer changed.
5. **Read it aloud, then test,** as the dialogue skill's Building step 7 says.
6. **Verify, then stop for review,** and add a project log entry.

## Outputs

In `stages/07-dialogue/output/<project>/`, each part with its marker (`[from: 07 step 3; dialogue-passes §1; voice §4]`):
- `draft-vN.md`, or one file per act, episode or chapter: the scenes with their talk, the number following on from stage 06's.

## Verify

In each draft file:
- **Against the action draft (stage 06):** each beat's line carries out the move marked for it, or the change is noted; the turn stays on the beat the card names.
- **Against the cast (stage 02):** nobody says what their "never says" column rules out, unless the scene is where it breaks, and says so; each voice passes the swap test (could the line go to anyone else?).
- **Against the plot (stage 04):** no line gives away a fact before its place in the reveal order.
- **Against the world (stage 03):** insiders keep to their shorthand; each term the audience must decode to follow the story has a way in, and shorthand it need not decode is left unexplained.

## Review stop

The writer reads the full draft. Next, usually: stage 08 on the draft, or on one act or episode at a time.
