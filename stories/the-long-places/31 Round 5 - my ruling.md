## Fix (4 findings)

1. **XIV: Emre's confession contradicts what Nilay remembers, and the text does not notice.** This is Fable's first finding. The people and continuity critics raised it this round, and the story critic in round 2; it was left as a quibble twice, and I now settle it.
   - *The problem:* in II no name is heard in the dark ("The humming did not change and did not stop, not for her word"), and XIV's narration agrees ("she had heard it once before, in a polished room"). Yet Emre says, "So I gave you the true answer instead … The name of the room."
   - *The answer the book already planted:* the lullaby "paused where the words would be" (II), and she "had never once let herself wonder what the words were" (II).
   - *The fix, two phrases in XIV:*
     - "she had heard it once before, in a polished room" becomes "she had heard it twice before, and known it once, in a polished room".
     - Emre's "So I gave you the true answer instead, the only one I had. The name of the room." becomes "So I gave you the true answer instead, the only one I had. The name of the room, in the place where the words go."
   - Nothing in II changes, and the drawer line at the end ("beside a tune that paused where the words would be") now closes too.
2. **XIII: the Ayios Nikandros wage-lines, on which the ending's cost depends, are too compressed to follow.** This is Fable's second finding. It raised the chapel in round 2 and the chapter's density in round 3; I left both for the audience test, and I now settle the narrow point.
   - XIV makes this page Nilay's burden ("the wage-line that stopped mid-column; whatever keeping weighed, and whatever its ceasing had cost, was hers now to carry"). A reader who has not understood what the page records has no floor under the ending.
   - *The fix:* one plain sentence straight after the wage-lines paragraph, for example: "Someone had taken a lamp out by boat to a drowned church every summer night for six years, on a wage nobody would put a name to, and then had stopped; and the List's line under the chapel's name said what came after: quiet."
   - Everything else in XIII stays.
3. **VII: the drilling dates break Melek's own rain rule.** Checker confirmed; I checked.
   - *The rule:* in II, Melek stops the 1999 search at the deep stair because "it had rained on the fifth; water pushes the old air up".
   - *The problem:* counting back from the camera lost on "the Thursday … the fourth of September" (the camera went down one day and "did the same the next day"), the drilling below the fourth door starts on Monday 1 September. That is the morning after the first rain since July (VI), with Melek sitting through it.
   - *The fix, VII:41:* "and did the same the next day" becomes "and did the same on the second lowering". The drilling then runs Tuesday to Thursday.
4. **XIV: the second handprint.** Checker confirmed. This one my own round-4 fix created: at the climax she presses her palm "beside the old print", and in the winter "there was one print, as there had always been".
   - *The fix, XIV:77:* "beside the old print" becomes "over the old print".
   - "The two prints sat together at the head of the counting, a thing and its shadow, and neither of them was first" then reads as one print laid over another, and the winter sentence stands.

**Also correct: three slips in my own round-4 wording, flagged by the planner:**
- VII:77: in the new strip sentence, cut "and did not average them". Chapter XI says Priska learned not to average "from a better man", after Márton's death, so she must not do it in September. The sentence ends "…she set the two figures side by side in the log."
- VII:77: "three hours and thirty-seven minutes" becomes "three hours and forty minutes". Those are Priska's own times, 10:04 to 13:44; 10:07 was Márton's.
- VIII:49: "None of mine deployed." becomes "None deployed by me." VII:79 has Yusuf pointing Márton's heat gun, so "none of mine" was untrue.
