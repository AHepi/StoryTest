## 1. Is time travel the very last plot point revealed?

The reveal is confined to Chapter XIV and lands in one accumulating sequence: Emre's explanation ("It's a road that's long instead of far"; "A place that's kept long enough stops being in only one of its times — it's in all of them, the way a road is in all its miles"; "The ones below are keepers too, from far along the road"), the sealed letter dated tomorrow ("You are reading a letter you have not written. You will write it today."), the C14 certificate ("Age: 10,720 ± 90 years before present."), the fingerprint match ("Friction ridges don't lie. 31 points, right hand."), and the line cut in Book Zero ("The keeper wrote this before she lived it."). The only occurrence of the phrase in the entire text is here: "two small clumsy words, *time travel*." Everything after that beat (the wall rendering, the closing keeper letter) compounds the same revelation rather than introducing a new plot point.

Nothing before it confirms. Every pre-ending candidate is explicitly forked: the impossible dates ("The room accepted this, the room having been in a satisfied mood all morning"; "backdating was the least mysterious practice in the province"), the drifting counts ("*ventilation* in the margin with a pleasure she did not stop to examine"), the clock ("*a clock is a donkey, you beat it and it walks*"), the watches (the manufacturer's advisory, the "committee"). The strongest pre-ending cluster — the letters dated before their favors ("Gratitude dated before the favor it thanked") — is left with standing mundane forks and never endorsed by narrator or character.

**PASS**

## 2. The four angles: hinted, near-canon, never confirmed; the dead never said outright

- **Fumes:** administratively canon and empirically supported — "The fumes were canon now, administratively, with signatures — the only doctrine in the valley that came with paperwork"; "on twenty-two nights before the masks, fifteen events; on twenty-six nights after, two" — yet undercut by "The cleanest event of the summer had happened in clean air" and the calibration splinter ("*considered indicative*"). Never confirmed as the whole answer.
- **Parallel reality:** Yusuf's near-canon theory with corroborating data — "The site's rendering two instances of itself — same map, two builds — and the sync's bad"; "The other instance. It loaded us"; the 1924/1998 chamber; the 41s sitting "inside a pooled-air window like a coin in a slot." Never confirmed.
- **Wormholes:** the full physics frame — "A TRAVERSABLE THROAT NEEDS NEGATIVE ENERGY: WHERE IS THE GENERATOR?"; Marques's "Your throat violates every energy condition that has ever bought us a theorem"; the 1997 elided word, "a hole through everything." Marques leaves "unmoved."
- **The dead:** leaned into hard — "The grandparents like company"; Havva's "I spoke with my mother. She was cross with me for the hour"; "Tell your Doctor the grandparents are restless"; "Forty years she carried the water down. Let it be poured for her now." But every instance is character speech or village idiom, held at fork's length by the narrator ("The company consoled; it did not inform") and finally re-keyed without erasure: "I believe it was somebody keeping." Never said outright, by narrator or doctrine.

**PASS**

## 3. Does the ending confirm time travel and tie the mysteries together?

Confirmed, and the text performs the re-keying itself: "the counts, the plans, the watches — true things, partial things; she let them go as true things go." Tied explicitly: Emre's fate, the shoulder-warmth, the humming ("There was a girl behind a stone once… 'It was me.' 'I know it now.'"), the ribbon, the varying counts, the plan discrepancy, the retarding clocks, the solstice watches, the Carpathian clocks, the deep chamber's occupants, the grain, the knocks, the Trust's foreknowledge ("thanking her for work she had not yet done, *in advance*" — the July-dated certificate was "in the building" before the vigil), the tape's answering voice, the opening letter's authorship.

Mysteries left untied (deliberate residue, consistent with rules 2 and 4 — no fix advisable):
- The triangle ("still eleven centimeters short, obedient as a mule") — covered by the mechanism only by implication.
- The vanished school party ("nothing ever has been found, and nothing ever will be") and the child's shoe.
- The 1951 vigil item ("plus one item: [inked out]").
- "There were two Twelfths, one cut over an erasure; she did not ask."
- The 14 seconds ("*viewed: no*"), permanently unwatched.
- What the Trust/Halden is ("The Register records the sites, Dr. Arat. It does not record the recorders.").
- Halime that night; Leyla Hanım's silence (human ambiguities, not site mysteries).
- The shaft's breathing shifting eighteen → nineteen → twenty minutes across chapters, unexplained.

**PASS**

## 4. The distant-future encounter, with never enough evidence

Present in Chapter VII and its aftermath: the finished chamber, the weave that "repeated nowhere," teeth "regular as a comb, in the manner of good prosthetic work; nothing else about him had ever been near a clinic," "Is the fire mountain awake?", the grain of "a variety with no standing field left in the world," the word that "did not sit in the mouth." Every evidentiary channel is destroyed or forked: media ("The day's folder came up zero bytes"), the lab ("The laboratory does not interpret zeros"; the grain "rejected as contamination; determination single; sample consumed"), and memory ("we supplied four witnesses to nothing"). The hermit theory "fit everything but the parts that fit nothing, which is why it would never die." Even the ending fixes only scale-free distance: "from far along the road," "It's been longer for me." The sole recoverable artifact stays unwatched forever. No reader can be confident what happened.

**PASS**

## 5. Sharp, concise questions about belief vs. science

Asked repeatedly and left standing: "When the mask works and the visitation stops — what did we cure?"; "If it can't be falsified, it isn't knowledge. It's comfort."; "Wanting is also a gas."; "It costs the question."; "Oxygen debt writes sermons, Priska. The theology is finished; the fumes remain."; "Science replaced the priest at the bedside. Who replaces the lullaby?"; "Would you want it explained, if explaining it ended it?"; the founder's three roads ("Known, a place becomes an asset… Believed, it becomes a banner… Left, it goes to the rain") and "pay the keepers"; "a belief is a keeping, kept."

**PASS**

## 6. Impact and consequence as shown theme; engineered dissociation

Shown through document-tracked consequence chains, never stated: the founder's 1949 economy ("I stopped the Nikandros remittances in a season of economies, as an economy") → "quiet since 1961" → "whatever keeping weighed, and whatever its ceasing had cost, was hers now to carry up the hill with the oil"; Yusuf's post → "past four million" → the fence → the cheapened knock → "the answers thinned"; Priska's fix → "Four percent… and nobody watched her"; Emre's errand — "which meant he meant to be gone ten minutes" — costing twenty-seven years. Dissociation is structural: the second-person break ("You wake where you sat down… You are reading a letter you have not written"), self-amending records ("the amendment was the truer memory and she had refused it on the night and it had taken the file by force"), "He remembered having decided. He could not remember deciding," and the wall that "began where it ended."

**PASS**

## 7. The villain: passive, quiet, unshowy, resourceful, formidable, original

A. Halden, Registrar — never seen doing anything. He pours tea "and drank none himself," wipes a thirty-year-old ring, arrives on foot. His reach is entirely through other hands and paper: sponsorship arranged "*in advance*" (Ch. I); consents collected in April (Ch. I); the closure memo "dated one day after the memo that cited it" answered by "Then you were anticipated, Doctor" (Ch. VIII); the solstice grant dated before the application (Ch. IX); the calibration splinter that guts Priska's provenance without touching her doctrine (Ch. X); "The transfer," he said, "was the least I could do" (Ch. X); the Carpathian acquittal delivered "For your peace" days before the death it plausibly enabled (Ch. IX); the warning that precedes the threat — "you are under no obligation to begin what you have not begun" (Ch. XII). His menace is registered as effect, not act: "Nothing in that room had happened, and she was afraid of it, and could not have written the sentence in any report on earth" (Ch. XIII). He is the story's own creation — an anchorite-registry clerk above a pharmacy with a possibly unbroken tenure ("The Trust has had two registrars") — sharing only Johan's grammar of menace, none of his history, appearance, or plot.

**PASS**
