## Things I noticed but did not change

The plan says not to touch anything else, so these are left for the main session to decide on.

- **VI, repeated opening.** After change 1, two sentences in a row start the same way: "By Saturday the counter went past a million, without a lip. By Saturday night it was past four million..." Both are correct for the new timeline, but the echo is new.
- **IX, which meter.** On the solstice night earlier in the chapter, Márton wears "Priska's spare meter". The new clause says "Priska's meter". A reader will probably take them as the same meter, but the wording is not identical.
- **IX, "dig-house".** The book writes both "dig house" and "dig-house". I used the plan's "dig-house door". That matches the chapter's own "dig-house wall", where the hyphen is also used before a noun.
