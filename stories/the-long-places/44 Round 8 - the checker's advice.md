## MUST FIX

**1. XIII:106. "…and my mother has waited twenty-six years."**
- **The problem.** Everywhere else, the book counts the years since 1999 by calendar year:
  - June 2025 is "Twenty-six years" at I:35, although only 25 years and 10 months had passed;
  - the 1998 survey has been "folded for twenty-seven years" in July 2025 (II:29);
  - "twenty-six years" comes back at II:39, II:67, II:77 and VII:43 as the 2025 refrain.

  XIII is a year later (Nilay has been "thirteen months in that country"; she lands on 3 September 2026). There, "twenty-six years" reads as the old refrain left unchanged, as if the book had lost a year. Then XIV opens two days later with "for twenty-seven years" (XIV:3, 31, 85). The number is exact to the day, as the plan argued. But a careful reader will not work that out, and the slip sits in the one new line the whole round was built around.
- **Smallest fix.** Change "twenty-six years" to "twenty-seven years". This matches the book's own way of counting and XIV's figures, and changes nothing else in the line. The ruling's text says "twenty-six", so this is the main session's call.

## QUIBBLES

**2. XIV:31 and XIV:51. The Hittite word has lost its last payoff.**
- **The problem.** The ordered cut of the aside also removed the one place that tied the far woman's three-syllable word (VII:63, VII:83, VIII:99) to the name of the room that Emre gives. The planner noted this (plan §4). The link now rests on the VII letter ("They give the name of their room"). The VII–VIII thread still earns its place through Demircioğlu's "late, and wrong, or early, and right", but the recognition that it was the same name in 1999, in 2025 and in the room is gone.
- **Fix, without touching the recognition beat.** In XIV:51, write «The name of the room — three syllables, the middle one long — in the place where the words go.» That adds six words, and the moment at "Emre" stays clean.

**3. XIII:35. "He was grayer by nothing" is cut.**
- **The problem.** The plan cut it as a repeat of IV:51. But in XIII it is not a repeat: it is the only sign that Halden has not aged in a year, one page before the 1949 photograph. The photograph still works, only with less build-up.
- **Fix.** Restore just «He was grayer by nothing.» (5 words) before "He poured the tea…". Leave out the repeated "dated and sound, the age of good paper".

**4. X:83. A run-on left by a cut.**
- **The problem.** The sentence now reads «…any more than a repeated office proves the thing it is about and it steadied her for the dark anyway…». The plan's seam has no comma, so the sentence stumbles.
- **Fix.** Add a comma after "about".

**5. VI:31. "those mornings" is ambiguous.**
- **The problem.** The sentence reads «Generator days, forty, forty, forty, and he made the excuses for those mornings himself…». With the list of excuses gone, "those mornings" now points back at the generator days, which gave forty. The excuses were really for the forty-one mornings.
- **Fix.** Change it to «for the forty-one mornings».

**6. XI:27 and XI:33. Two nouns lost what they referred to.**

- **Fix.**
  - Change "the list" to «his prices laminated», or to «the price list laminated».
  - Change "the explanations" to «the ordinary explanations».
