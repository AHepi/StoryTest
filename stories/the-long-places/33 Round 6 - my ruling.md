## Fix (4 findings)

1. **XIV:37: why the boy did not follow the grey man up at dawn.** This is the checker's one confirmed finding, and I reverse my round-1 call.
   - In round 1 I accepted that the grey light "need not reach" the low corridor. But II:65 shows that it does ("The grey came down the corridor thin as skimmed milk, and the stone took one finger"). So the taken light does not explain it.
   - *The fix:* "and in the morning he went up and took the light with him" becomes "and in the morning, while I slept, he went up and took the light with him".
   - Nothing else changes; "Keepers leave you the lamp" keeps its weight.
2. **IX to X: did Márton go below past Melek's "first cloud"?** The story and continuity critics raised it this round, and the continuity critic in round 1. The checker calls it a quibble but says "the point has come back since round 1, and a clause would settle it"; by my rule, I settle it.
   - Priska came home "the evening before, in snow, past his window, and the window had been dark", so he had gone down before the snow.
   - *The fix:* one sentence in X after the 0.4 reading, for example: "The snow had come on after he went down; his sky had held at the mouth. Melek said so at the step, and nothing else."
3. **VII: the tall woman "bent to" a doorway that is in the chamber's ceiling.** Fable's third finding, and my own round-1 fix caused it.
   - The new-cut doorway is in the floor of the low room above, so from inside the polished room it is overhead ("the block of cut tuff went back into its socket above them"). You cannot bend to a hole in your ceiling.
   - Keep the scale clue I added in round 1 and Márton's forearm arithmetic in VIII (they are the book's one hint of deep time in the figures). Make the geometry agree: "and kept rising, or the low ceiling made it seem so, and bent to the new-cut doorway as to a child's door" becomes "and kept rising, or the low ceiling made it seem so, until the new-cut doorway was at her shoulder".
   - "…and measured it with her forearm, elbow to fingertip, twice" follows unchanged.
4. **IX:37: Marques speaks Márton's private joke from III as her own.** From Fable's first finding.
   - "Three are a committee" was Márton's own thought in III ("Three are a committee, and a committee can be outvoted"). Marques says it in IX with no sign she is quoting him.
   - *The fix:* make it a quotation of what he once wrote to her: "Two clocks is an anecdote. Three are a committee, you wrote me once. Committees can be bribed by warmth, and always are."

**Also correct (two slips from round 5):**
- XIV:31: "she had heard it twice before, and known it once, in a polished room" reads as though both hearings were in the polished room. It becomes "she had heard it twice before, once in the dark without knowing it, and once, knowing it, in a polished room".
- VII:77: "the strip ran as long as the watches, three hours and forty minutes". Three hours forty are Priska's own times, 10:04 to 13:44; the watches gave 13:44 against Márton's 10:07. It becomes "the strip ran as long as her own times, three hours and forty minutes".
