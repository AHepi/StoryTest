## 1. The reunion: let Nilay ask him to come

**The fault, in the skill's terms:** Emre's staying is the book's last big choice, and it has only one option on the page. Nilay never asks. So "of course he did not follow" settles a question the scene never raised, and her becoming able to leave happens off the page.

**The change:** somewhere between "Mother is alive," she said. "She keeps one room." (XIV:53) and "She went up into the grey." (XIV:83), Nilay asks him to come up with her, to their mother. The request must be real: she has just found the brother whose absence organised her life, and her mother is alive one floor of hill above. **His answer must make her departure possible,** so that the reader sees the moment she becomes able to leave him there.

**Limits:**
- Build only from what the page already holds:
  - his first winter ("The mouth opened on a morning I couldn't read. Strangers' smoke. A dog I didn't know.");
  - his cuts on the wall, "whichever year his year was";
  - the ribbon in her lap;
  - "Small and exact";
  - the good distance;
  - "The room chose you";
  - "I never do";
  - keeping as both care and withholding.
- Keep the road uncertain. No new rule, no law, no fact about where or when the mouth would let him out, beyond what he already says. The owner's concept keeps the mechanism unknown ("I don't know the law").
- Add no new fact about Halden, and nothing about the far-future people.
- Emre speaks plainly, with the keepers' grammar surfacing, as now. Nilay asks in her own voice with the polish gone, as she did with Halden in XIII.
- "of course he did not follow" must be earned or changed. The words "I never do," and the quotation they make, stay.
- Leave the recognition beat (XIV:29–33), the lines listed above, and the mother's ribbon scene untouched. The addition should make clearer what Nilay brings back (the ribbon, and her word from him) and what she leaves (him).
- Between 120 and 300 words added. It is a small addition, not a new scene.

## 3. Give the actions more room before explaining their meaning

A selective pass over the whole book, outside the italic letters.

**What to look for:** narration that certifies what a scene means, or whether someone behaved correctly, after the action has already shown it. Examples:
- "and it was the correct whole";
- "and the not-arguing was the breaking";
- "that was all";
- "it was enough";
- "the whole of the teaching";
- the aphoristic paragraph-closer that settles rescue, bereavement, evasion and tenderness in the same cadence.

**Two rules decide each case:**
1. **Keep the repetitions that change meaning when they return:** the good distance, the oil to the knuckle, the names of rooms, the shaft's breath, "Nothing to record", "kept".
2. **Cut or trim the commentary where the action has already carried the meaning.** If the action has not carried it, the line stays. Give the page reason for each keep and each cut.

**Priority:** the narration around the strongest scenes (the five above, the breach, Melek's confession, Márton's last descent, the reunion). Include these lines Fable named in round 8 (file 45, quibbles 1 and 2):
- the doubled "not ambush, not accident" (VII);
- "the door of the sentence shut as softly as it opened" (V);
- "the rooms receiving them anyway, the way stone receives rain" (VI);
- "the nothing had room in it" given to Márton and again to Yusuf;
- "Both texts were true; the page held one" (V).

**There is no quota.** Expect to cut roughly half of the certifying endings, and never touch a refrain or a plant. Each cut is a phrase or a sentence; no scene is cut.

**How:** a planner writes a ledger of every candidate, with KEEP, CUT or TRIM and its page reason, and exact find-and-replace edits as in round 8. I check the ledger. Three revisers apply it by block.

## 4. The final documents: completing Nilay's recognition, not independent proof

**I decide for recognition,** and soften the absolute claim. My reasons:
- The book has taught us to distrust exactly this institution's control of evidence.
- Fable asks that the proof stay Halden's paper.
- The owner's concept needs time travel confirmed at the end, and Emre's plain sentence plus the documents still do that for the reader.
- The loop is kept by Nilay choosing not to fork this one measurement, not by the paper being unforkable.

**The change:** "It was the one measurement taken all year that no fork could carry, and she held it the way you hold a lit thing: cupped, and low." becomes, for example, "It was the one measurement all year she did not try to fork, though it had come to her, like every other, through his hands; and she held it the way you hold a lit thing: cupped, and low."
- The planner may tighten the wording.
- It must keep three things: that the paper came through the Trust; that she chose not to fork it; and "cupped, and low".
- The documents themselves, the certificate, the chit and its provenance clause, stay word for word.
