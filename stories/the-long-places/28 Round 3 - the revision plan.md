No change is planned for any finding the ruling overruled, left for the audience test, or left as a quibble (Fable 1 and 2, Story 1, People 1, MiMo 1 to 4, and all of Fable's and the critics' quibbles).

Line numbers refer to those files as they stand now. Every "Find" string below was checked and occurs exactly once in its file. Every replacement was also tried on a copy in memory, to check that the result reads correctly in place.

**Rules for the revisers**

- Change only the words named. Do not touch any other sentence, even a neighbouring one that looks improvable.
- Copy each "Replace with" text exactly, character for character. All four chapters that change use straight apostrophes (`'`) and straight double quotes (`"`); the dash is the em dash (—); italics are `*...*`.
- Do not edit the round-2 files, the critiques, the ruling, or anything under `/home/user/StoryTest/stories/the-long-places`.
- The fumes stay almost canon and never confirmed. Only V's two sentences name or picture the masks differently. Every other mention of masks stays exactly as it is: V:63 ("When the mask works"), V:67's later "mandated the masks", V:69, VI:31 (Márton's forty-one "mask or no"), VII:77, VIII:111 and 113, and IX:59 (Priska's "cartridges" and "Wear the mask below the second door"). Do not add "scrubber", "soda-lime" or "canister" anywhere else.
- The found letter's line "*and I did not cry until the ribbon.*" (XIV:19) stays word for word. Emre's scene and the mother's scene (XIV:107) stay exactly as they are.
- Net length change for the book: +29 words on 49,439 (+0.06%). No chapter moves by more than 0.4%.
