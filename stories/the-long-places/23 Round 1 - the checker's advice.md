## Confirmed findings

1. **The knock's answers thin before they begin** (XI, lines 27–29; see also lines 33 and 35). Story, People, Continuity.
2. **The fumes theory is confirmed in the reveal chapter**: "Priska's air had been true too… carrying the small mercies" (XIV, line 75; against V line 87 and X line 39). Story.
3. **"The dead dug upward" names the dead outright**, twice, in the keepers' voice (VIII, lines 9 and 29). Story.
4. **The sealed-hour proof miscounts its sheets**: three sheets, "Both sheets went back into the tin box" (XIV, lines 19, 89, 97). Story, Continuity.
5. **The mother never appears after the reunion, and Nilay's own coming departure costs nothing on the page** (XIV, from line 51 to the end; set up in I line 35, II line 69, XIII lines 33 and 126, XIV line 3). Story, People.
6. **Halime is cleared by the reveal but never released** (II line 67; XI line 71; XIV lines 43–47). Story, People.
7. **The distant-future humans read as ancient; Emre's "Farther than I've been… farther back" steers readers to the past, and almost nothing signals astronomical scale** (VII lines 61–75; VIII lines 43 and 101; XII line 9; XIV line 67). Story, MiMo.

Total confirmed: 7. One of them (no. 7) came from MiMo, shared with the story critic. MiMo's other three SUBSTANTIVE findings were WRONG.
