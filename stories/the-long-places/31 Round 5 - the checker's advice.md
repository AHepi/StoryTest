## Confirmed findings

1. **The drilling days against Melek's rain rule (VII, with VI and II).**
   - If the camera was lost on the second lowering day, a Thursday, the rig started on Monday 1 September. That is the morning after the first rain since July, with water still standing in the gallery.
   - Melek sits below the fourth door through all of it, against her own "dry days only" and against the rule she used in 1999 to turn the search back.
   - The critic's fix is minimal. At VII:41, "and did the same the next day" becomes "and did the same on the second lowering". Then the hollow, the sounding and the loss all fall on Thursday the fourth, drilling runs Tuesday to Thursday, and "sounded 4.ix" is true.
   - Chapters: VII (lines 33–43). Checked against VI:41–43 and II:49.

2. **The second handprint (XIV).**
   - At XIV:77 Nilay presses her palm "beside" the old print, and "the two prints sat together".
   - At XIV:127 the winter wall shows "one print, as there had always been", and she cannot say "which of the two it was". I:63 lists a single print in 1924.
   - The two lines from the same visit do reach her wall. That asymmetry is story 3's point, and it has the same cause.
   - Making XIV:77 read "over the old print" (and "sat one in the other") settles both, and leaves XIV:127 exactly as it is.
   - Chapter: XIV (lines 77 and 127). Checked against I:63.
