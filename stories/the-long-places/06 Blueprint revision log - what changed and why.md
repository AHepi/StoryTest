## Changes made to keep it coherent

- **The Halden method change rippled furthest.** Replacing mirroring with precocity required re-dating documents in I, VI, VIII, IX, and XI, and re-choosing Nilay's two named hinges in XIII so that the reader sees a pattern no character names. The deleted creed's content had to live somewhere; it moved to Lambrick's unsent 1949 letter, which required creating the Ayios Nikandros chapel — which in turn serves the kept-but-quiet counter-example. One fiction now carries three fixes.
- **Seher consolidates four fixes into one person** — the fourth theory's carrier, the widow whose masked vigil gets nothing, Priska's best antagonist, and the witness who names the cost of keeping. Adding four minor characters would have bloated the cast; one grieving schoolteacher threads V, X, and XI instead.
- **The palmprint reposition** (head of the sequence, not the last marks) changed three texts — the Ch. I survey line, the Ch. IV vial label, and Emre's XIV clause — and added a stratigraphy note to rule 11.
- **The Ch. VII demotions propagated**: rule 7's permitted-description list changed (textiles, teeth, diction, footprints; thermal only as Yusuf's memory), the ledger changed (19, 23, 24, 28), and §7's supersession gained the edges clause plus an explicit consignment of residue.
- **The spoken-question cap forced redistribution**: Melek's undecided death (XI), Halden's written paragraph (VIII), the deletion moment (XII), Lambrick's letter and remittances (XIII), the self-unknowing gesture (XIV).
- **The mother being alive** rippled to II (Kayseri), V (in the village, denying the lullaby was ever hers alone), and XIV (Emre's jaw is a living woman's jaw).
- Interludes V, VIII, X, and XII rewritten; the interlude list, rule 18, and rule 9 updated to match. Ch. XII retitled. POV arithmetic corrected throughout (Nilay: 8; the others: 2 each). The dossier rebuild of VIII absorbed the funeral vigil, so craft fixes 8 and 9 share one chapter without crowding.

## Still unresolved

- **Emre remains guessable.** Genre-literate readers may still call it by mid-book; the mitigations lower their confidence, not the possibility. Accepted as the price of the grief engine — the alternative (no brother, no lullaby) guts the book.
- **Ch. XIV remains the risk chapter.** Breathing room was bought with re-sequencing and +200 words; the density persists.
- **The tide and wrist devices may still fire under threshold** for some readers. Flagging helps; detection rates vary.
- **Ayios Nikandros slightly dilutes the engine's elegance.** Accepted trade for thematic openness. If the owner prefers a canon engine, deleting one line re-rigs the book toward theodicy — flagged as a live decision point, not settled here.
- **Halden's precocity can read as supernatural** rather than administrative. Three readings are deliberately live. If a fully mundane Halden is wanted, the backdating reading must be strengthened in draft (a character half-noticing a postmark).
- **By-design loose ends** — the 0.1 m datum, the full cup, the coin, the doubled Twelfth mark, the inked-out item, the 1949 photograph — will read as loose to some readers. That is the cost of the kept-uncertainty discipline.
- **The second-person page in XIV** is a tonal hazard, kept because the slip needs a body.
- **The social texture (tours, widow, viewer)** risks softening XIII's dread; pacing is controlled in outline and must be watched in draft.
- **Whether Nilay publishes** is intentionally left open. The book ends before the choice; so does this blueprint.
