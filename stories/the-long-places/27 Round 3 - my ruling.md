## Fix (4 findings)

1. **The masks cannot do what the book says they did (V, with IX).** Checker confirmed; I checked.
   - *The problem:* dust masks and filter cartridges do not stop carbon dioxide. Only a scrubber (soda lime) or supplied air does. Yet Priska, the book's authority on exactly this gas, prescribes the masks against it, and V:89 calls them masks "against dust".
   - *Why it matters:* an informed reader is given a reason to throw out the one explanation that carries an official stamp. That tips the balance the owner asked for (fumes almost canon, never confirmed), and it makes Priska act against her own science.
   - *The fix:* name the kind once and let everything else stand.
     - V:67: "and masks below the second door on sitting nights" becomes "and scrubber masks, soda-lime canisters of the mine-rescue kind, below the second door on sitting nights".
     - V:89: "wearing masks against dust, like women at a threshing" becomes "wearing their masks the way women at a threshing wear cloths against the chaff".
     - IX's "cartridges" then reads as the canisters and stays.

2. **XIV: the found letter's one prediction, "and I did not cry until the ribbon", is never shown coming true.** Fable's third finding. The story and people critics raised it in round 1, and I left it then as restraint. I now reverse that, for Fable's reason.
   - The narration marks this line as the one thing on the sheet she "has not lived yet", which makes it the loop's one prediction, and the chapter never shows it kept. A careful reader watches for it and is left unsure whether the letter was right, which is the opposite of the letter's job.
   - *The fix:* one sentence, not a scene, straight after the ribbon is described ("…faded the way twenty-seven years fade a thing, or longer."): "She cried then, as the sheet had said, and not before."
   - Nothing else changes; the mother's scene keeps its own weight.

3. **VIII:95: the lamp summary quotes a statement that does not exist.** Checker confirmed; I checked.
   - The row reads "The lamp: the stair head; the niche; the headlamp; *they stood where they were put*", but no statement says that. Melek's statement says "Mine stayed, filled, in the niche", the same lamp as Nilay's.
   - In the chapter whose method is exact comparison, the narrator misquotes the documents just above.
   - *The fix:* make the fourth item Melek's actual answer: "The lamp: the stair head; the niche; the headlamp; the niche again, *filled*."

4. **V: Priska's father's cough lasts twelve years, then thirty.** Checker confirmed. V:29 says "she was fourteen when the cough began and twenty-six when it ended". V:55 says "Thirty years of it". Change V:55's "Thirty years of it" to "Twelve years of it". (The continuity critic caught this in round 1 too, and I wrongly left it among the quibbles.)

**Also correct (a garbled phrase):** IX:53, "her clipboard steaming coffee", becomes "her clipboard in one hand and her coffee steaming in the other".
