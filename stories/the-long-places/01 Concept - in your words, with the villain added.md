# The concept, in the owner's words

Write a story about the following concept: when man made places like: shrines, cave networks, temples, ritual sites, exist for incredible amounts of time, they suddenly inherit a mystical quality that allows people that inhabit those places to travel through time. The time travel element must be the very last plot point to be revealed. The story should lean into the idea that they might be supernatural gateways to contact the dead without ever actually saying that. Some people think these places expose people to toxic fumes. Some people think they're gateways into parallel reality. Yet others think they may be wormholes. All angles must be hinted at, and almost established as cannon, but never confirmed. The confirmation comes at the very end when it's finally confirmed that time travel is the thing that ties all the mysteries together.
The point is to ask sharp and concise questions about the role of belief and spirituality in society. Can science really ever replace belief, or is there something about it that simply cannot be substituted.

Also add a sci-fi element somewhere. An anomalous situation where our characters encounter humans from the astronomically distant future, but don't really understand what they are experiencing and why. There can never be enough evidence for a reader to ever feel confident that they know what's happening.

The theme should revolve around impact and consequence. The reader should experience mind bending dissociation.

# The owner's added qualification

The main villain must be of the same quality as Johan Liebert from the anime Monster. Passive, quiet, non show stopping, but remarkably resourceful and a formidable threat.

(Note for the writers, added by the orchestrator: "of the same quality" means a villain who works on a reader the way Johan does: quiet, patient, rarely seen doing anything, reaching through other people, and more frightening for it. He or she must be this story's own creation, not a copy of Johan's history, looks or plot.)
