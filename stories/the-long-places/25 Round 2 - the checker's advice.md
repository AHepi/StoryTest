# Round 2 verification: the SUBSTANTIVE findings checked against the book

**What I read.** I read the owner's concept, all fourteen round-1 chapter files in full and all four critiques. MiMo's critique (`critique-mimo.md`) was there when I started. I also read the round-1 ruling so that I could tell when a finding repeats one already settled. These verdicts are advice; the main Claude session makes the final ruling.

**Method.** I checked every finding a critic marked SUBSTANTIVE against the chapter text. Line numbers refer to the round-1 chapter files. I merged duplicates, including two items that one critic marked QUIBBLE and another marked SUBSTANTIVE. I did not re-rule findings that every critic marked QUIBBLE; the notes after the table cover them.

- **CONFIRMED:** the problem is real, and substantive by the definition.
- **QUIBBLE:** the problem is real, but only a quibble.
- **WRONG:** the finding is not true of the book.

## Notes on findings marked QUIBBLE by every critic (not re-ruled)

I agree they are quibbles. Two points are worth passing on.

- **People 13** ("Melek did not cross herself") rests on a partly wrong premise. The village has a church: the grandmother set her clock "by the church bell" (I:57), and there is "the village church, nave, not towers" (III:47). The line is less foreign to the setting than the critic says.
- **Continuity 11 and MiMo 4** were marked QUIBBLE by their critics but duplicate SUBSTANTIVE findings. They are merged into rows 4 and 8.

## CONFIRMED findings, numbered

1. **The sealed-hour procedure: what the wax seals, the order of sealing and stopping the watch, and the uncounted sheets.** Chapters XIII (the protocol, lines 83–90) and XIV (lines 5, 13, 15, 19, 89). From Story 1(a) and Continuity 1.
2. **The found sheet dated "tomorrow" when it must be dated today, the fifth.** Chapter XIV (line 19, against lines 21, 89 and 97). From Story 1(b) and Continuity 4.
3. **Priska lets Nilay sit a pooled-air night below the second door without her meter, and the text never accounts for it.** Chapter XIV (lines 13–15 and 83), set up in VI (line 41), IX (lines 51, 59, 79) and X (lines 39, 79). From Story 2.
4. **"Nobody counted" contradicts the counting in the same scene and paragraph.** Chapter VII (lines 57, 61, 71). From Story 3, with Continuity 11.
5. **Halden's parcel, his card and his visit on the last evening vanish at the IX/X seam, although X uses the parcel's contents.** Chapters IX (lines 65–75) and X (lines 43, 45, 53, 73). From People 1 and Continuity 5.
6. **"did not argue", followed by an argument, at the moment Nilay and Priska part.** Chapter X (lines 61–71). From People 5.
