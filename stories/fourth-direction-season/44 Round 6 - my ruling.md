## Fix (9 findings, each a line or a phrase)

1. **Episode 6, scene 6: Tess's clinic crossing breaks the season's own rule that the way back takes as long as the way in.** Fable 1.
   - She stands "on the pavement by the black door", goes down a floor and in a wall to the cabinet at 2.2 seconds, then is back on the pavement by 2.6.
   - *The fix:* start her at lower-ground level. Harley Street houses have a sunken area with steps down.
     - In Rosie's briefing in the same episode, add "Area steps. Their door's below the pavement."
     - Tess "goes down the area steps and stands by the lower-ground wall" instead of on the pavement, and steps out into the area at the end instead of "onto the pavement".
     - The crossing is then one wall in, the cabinet, one wall out: about 2.5 seconds, as she planned.
2. **Episodes 1, 6, 7: "Twice. Under three." could not have reached the Mayfair shelf.** Fable 2. The shelf is nearly five seconds in from the wall she went through. Change the alternative, not the route (Fable's option a):
   - *Episode 1:* Liam's "Could've gone in twice. Quick in, quick out. Wall never floods." becomes "Could've gone in twice. Quick in, quick out. Half the beads."
   - *Episode 6:* Tess's "*Twice. Under three.*" becomes "*Twice. Four and four.*"
   - Anything in episodes 7 or 8 that repeats "under three" as the safer split changes to match. At four seconds the table gives one in twelve each, against three in five for 9.4, so the guilt stays exact.
3. **Episode 7, scene 3: Tess opens a two-week-old note on the very morning of its date.** MiMo 1. The note must already have been opened.
   - "She opens it with her thumbnail: one slit, clean." becomes "She takes it out, already slit, and reads it again."
   - Her line "Two weeks I've had that in my pocket" stays true: she has sat on its contents, which is her flaw.
4. **Episode 9: "You went in the Deep" is given to Rosie, and Peg then says it back to her.** MiMo 5.
   - Cut the first line: "ROSIE: *(from the stove end, in the big jumper, dry now)* You went in the Deep."
   - Move its stage direction onto Rosie's "I went in a sea, Nan."
5. **Episode 10: Frank's September 2001 tickets are read off "the 2002 roll".** The continuity critic raised it in round 1 and MiMo now confirms it. I left it in round 1; the boxes are calendar years ("the one marked 02 … your dad's year"), so it gets settled.
   - "Tess unrolls the 2002 roll" becomes "Tess unrolls the last of the 2001 roll and the 2002 roll after it, end to end".
6. **Epilogue: the recruiter's card is "still standing where she left it in June", but in a different hospital.** MiMo 6. It stood on the Whitechapel locker in June; the epilogue is at the Royal Free. Change the phrase to "the Fever Company card, brought with her from Whitechapel".
7. **Episodes 1 and 10: Terry's "last ticket, 70.6" is framed, but it was never printed on screen.** MiMo 7. In episode 1 Terry never steps on the plate, and the plan has this very shot. In episode 10, the night before he goes, Terry steps onto the little bridge for the first time in the season. The printer chatters, and Kieran tears off the ticket: 70.6. It is wordless, or at most one line.
8. **Episode 7: "Not one under three seconds" is said in the room with Danny, who turned at 2.2.** MiMo 8. It was left as a quibble in round 2, and it has come back. After "Not one, either side of the river, not in twenty-five years", Tess adds, for example, "Bar Danny, and that was the grip, not the dice."
9. **Episodes 1 and 10: Tess's hunger at fourteen never meets her son's.** MiMo 2; the people critic raised it in round 1.
   - In episode 1, Kieran asks "How old were you?" and Tess says "It was a different week" and "doesn't say which week".
   - In episode 10, at the night of writing, one short exchange: Kieran asks again, and this time she tells him which week, for example: "The week your grandad went. Nan stood me on that plate and everybody clapped, and I was hungry." (It echoes what she told Rosie in episode 2, in the family's own words.)
   - No more than that.
