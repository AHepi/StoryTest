### Episode 6

1. **Scene 5 (the briefing), finding 1.** Rosie's casing line ends: "…And on the wall by the toilet, one of them steel panels. Like the one in their lock-up. Area steps. Their door's below the pavement."
2. **Scene 5, finding 1.** "Tess is already walking it, lips moving: pavement, down a floor, half left, first door." becomes "Tess is already walking it, lips moving: area steps, one wall, half left, first door." ("Cabinet on the left going in." / "The wall you face coming out." stand.)
3. **Scene 6, scene setting, finding 1.** "A man walks a terrier along the railings." becomes "A man walks a terrier along the area railings."
4. **Scene 6, the approach, finding 1.** "She gets out, crosses, and stands on the pavement by the black door like a woman waiting to be late for an appointment." becomes "She gets out, crosses to the railings, and goes down the area steps to the lower-ground wall, where she stands like a woman waiting to be late for an appointment."
5. **Scene 6, the black, finding 1.** The block is replaced whole. Old: "…and she drops by count, the way you drop a floor into a vault. / 0.4. Stone steps from the inside. 0.9. A coal chute, a mouse run in old lath. The glow slides her down through the dark of the house. 1.3. A void, warm… 1.7. The cabinet… / 2.1. The wall counts to three… / 2.2. She draws the PROTOCOL binder… / She faces it and steps out onto the pavement. / A gasp. Exhaust, cold, the hiss of a night bus. The door of the van, and Terry's window down." New:
   - "…and she goes straight in, level, one wall, the way you'd walk from a passage into a kitchen.
   - 0.4. The area wall from the inside: brick, then a coal chute, a mouse run in old lath. 0.9. The lower corridor, warm, and the shapes in it: a desk from the inside, its lock, a lamp's wiring lying in the wood like veins. On the wall a plain steel panel the size of a door, with nothing on it to read. It doesn't care what her budget is. It counts to three.
   - 1.2. The cabinet. Steel, floor-bolted, tall as Terry, exactly as Danny said, and through its door the spines stand clear as through water: LEDGER A–F. LEDGER G–M. SUPPLIERS. KITCHENS. PROTOCOL.
   - 1.6. The wall counts to three, and the wall doesn't count her change.
   - 1.7. She draws the PROTOCOL binder out through the steel, the way Rosie drew the canvas out of its crate, and holds it flat against her chest.
   - No fizz in her nails. Home is one pull, the strongest thing in the world. She faces it, goes back the way she came, and steps out into the area.
   - A gasp. Exhaust, cold, the hiss of a night bus overhead. She comes up the area steps, round the corner to the van, and Terry's window is down."
6. **Scene 9 (the sums), finding 2.** "Her lips over both: *Twice. Under three.*" becomes "Her lips over both: *Twice. Four and four.*"
7. **No other numbers move, finding 1.** Osei's "Two point six seconds. Sixty-one kilos, seven hundred grams." in scene 8 stands, as does the episode's numbers note ("2.5 planned, 2.6 and 61.7 on the clinic's wall"): planned 2.5, wall 2.6, one wall in and the same wall out.
