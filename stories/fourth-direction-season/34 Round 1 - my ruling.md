**The bar:**
- A finding is fixed if a careful viewer would notice it and it weakens the season, or if it breaks the owner's rules or the plan's.
- Each fix is as small as the problem allows: a line, a clause, or one short exchange. No new scenes.
- Everything that works stays. Everything not listed is left alone, and quibbles are left for the audience test.

### Breaches of the plan's rules

Cut the cylinder from both action lines: "on a riverbank with a cylinder behind him" becomes "on a riverbank", and "a cylinder behind him" goes in episode 10. Nothing else in either shot changes.
2. **Episode 10, scene 10: an instrument reacts live to an unseen crosser.** Fable, quibble 3. I rule it substantive, because the plan's "Never echo The Catch" rule says "No instrument showing an unseen visitor live, anywhere: every such reading is found on the roll afterwards." Cut the sentence "Under the counter the printer chatters once more, unprompted, and nobody looks at it."
3. **Episode 10, scenes 11 and 12: Terry steps off the world with no rig on him.** MiMo 1, confirmed; I checked. Rosie is "rigged in her aunt's old belt"; Terry is washed and his clothes folded, and he goes. The rig is the field. Add one silent beat after Peg washes his back: she buckles his belt on him and clips the collar. No dialogue.
4. **Episode 10, scenes 11 and 12: Rosie crosses "empty, holding nothing", then has "her held breath in her chest".** Continuity 3, confirmed; I checked. In the black on the shore she takes one breath of their air and holds it, as she did in the sea in episode 9. The rest stands, including "lets her last breath out — hers, only hers, into our air".

### Who knows what, and when

5. **Episode 7: Tess remembers and quotes Danny's Mynors boast, made at the bank in episode 2 when she was not there.** Fable 1; I checked. Tess is at the table in episode 2, scene 6.
   - *Episode 2, scene 6:* after "Banks. Banks I've never heard of.", add the boast at the table in Danny's words, for example "Mynors had us back last autumn for the floor under the one we did in '24. Two floors now. Long time to be nowhere."
   - *Episode 7:* make the flashback be of that table, Tuesday evening, not the bank yard.
   - Keep the bank scene in episode 2 as it is: Liam's pink ears are doing their own job.
6. **Episode 7, scene 12: Rosie's confession tells the family what only Maggie told Tess on the ferry.** Continuity 1, confirmed; I checked. Rosie's line "Their lad was six metres off with a stopwatch, timing me, and I never knew him from a pull in my gut." becomes "There was never anybody near me. I never felt a thing."
7. **Episode 7, scene 11: Tess quotes Maggie saying something Maggie never says:** "She'd walked it since she was fifteen, you said. Every job in it six seconds." This is Fable's quibble 4, and I rule it substantive because it is the same knowledge slip as 5 and 6, at the same hinge. Give Maggie that history in her own words earlier on the ferry, during the counting (scene 9 or where the two women read jobs aloud), so Tess's "you said" is true.

### The season's own numbers

8. **Episode 7, scene 4: Lee's weights cannot give the nine and a half seconds the scene says.** Fable 2; I checked. By the painted rule, a fifth of a second per kilo under a hundred, 66 kilos buys 6.8 seconds.
   - Keep the shape of Lee's curve (a fall, the long job at Canary Wharf, then a rise) and "Nine and a half … same as Mayfair, near enough".
   - Lower the weights so that his weight on the Canary Wharf job gives nine and a half seconds by the rule: 52.5 kilos or less. For example, starting near 64 in November, falling to 52 by February, and rising after.
   - Change every mention of Lee's weights anywhere in the season to match.
9. **Episode 7, scene 12: Tess offers the ten-second cure to Terry and Danny, who cannot take ten seconds.** Fable 4; I checked. Terry's budget is about 5.7 seconds and Danny's 2.2. The woman who never gets a sum wrong out loud makes a plain error nobody names. Her line becomes, for example, "Rosie. Tonight. Terry at five, and again, and again. Danny—" and she stops.
10. **Episode 10, scene 6: the depot wall's log misdescribes the raid we watched.** Continuity 7, confirmed; I checked. Every leg carried a case. The investigator's line becomes "One of them nine times, three kilos each go. The other twice, the same." Also cut "Both men.": a wall gives a weight, not a sex.

### Rules said and then broken without a word

Nothing else changes; the dog's choice of the second case then lands as the price of that line.

13. **Episodes 5, 9 and 10: the twenty-year law for stepping out on purpose is stated twice, broken three times, and charged to nobody.** Continuity 8 and MiMo 3, confirmed. At the station (episode 10), after "And their sea went down my drain. Write that an' all.", Peg adds one line owning it, for example "And my girl stepped out on purpose. Twice. Put it with the rest."

### Plants and payoffs

14. **Episode 1 and episode 9: why Rosie cannot fire her main field in the sea is never planted.** Fable 5. The story critic called the related point a quibble; I rule it substantive. At her most important rule-break most viewers will ask why she doesn't just press it, and the answer has to be derived in the dark.
    - *The plant, episode 1, the pit:* after "There's nowhere to fall.", add one exchange. KIERAN: "What if you pressed it in here?" TESS: "Then you'd be carrying the pit with you. Water counts. Everything counts."
    - Nothing in episode 9 changes.
15. **Episodes 6, 7 and 9: Tess hears Osei's wall read 61.7 against her bridge's 61.0, files it, and never uses it.** Story 4, confirmed. This is the 1.1 per cent that killed Liam, and she holds it for three episodes while Danny asks "And Liam?". Give her one line in character, at the stove in episode 7 or when the scale is proven in episode 9, for example: "Their wall had me at sixty-one seven. I told myself their wall was wrong."
16. **Episodes 4, 5, 9 and 10: Rosie's second secret, that she taught Liam to make weight, is never said.** People 2, confirmed. The season says every other secret aloud. At the night of writing in episode 10, when Liam's line goes onto the roll, Rosie says her half, for example: "Nan put the plate in front of him. I'm the one taught him to be hungry for it." One line, and no reply is needed.
17. **Episodes 6 and 7: the two-week gap is unmarked, and the ferry note has no date.** Story 11 and MiMo 2, confirmed. Tess seems to be in two places at once, and she arrives on Maggie's Thursday by luck.
    - Caption episode 7's cold open ("Two weeks later", or "Thursday fortnight").
    - Put the date on the note ("THURSDAY 21ST").
    - Add one line from Tess on the way to the ferry, for example "Two weeks I've had that in my pocket."
18. **Episode 10, scene 8: "Rosie did it in three. You'll take longer." is said at two in the morning about a step still five hours away.** MiMo 5, confirmed. Put it in the future and name what she finds: "Rosie'll find the pull in three. You'll take longer."
