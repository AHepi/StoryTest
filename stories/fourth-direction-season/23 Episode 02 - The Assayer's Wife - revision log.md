# Episode 2, "The Assayer's Wife": revision log

I left the draft (`80-episode-02-draft.md`) as it was. The revised episode is `82-episode-02-final.md`. I checked every finding in the critique (`81-episode-02-critique.md`) against the master plan (`70-plan-v3.md`) and the draft. Since the critique was written, episode 1 has been finished (`82-episode-01-final.md`), so I also checked each point against that finished episode. Where the two episodes touch, the finished episode 1 wins.

Every line the critique asked me to protect is kept word for word. I added no new rule and no new canonical number, and none of the plan's banned words reaches the screen.

## Other changes I made, and why

- **Tess's reaction to "New roll's printed backwards".** In the draft, her face was "completely open" for a second, which suggested she was afraid for Rosie. The plan has Tess suspect nothing until episode 3. Now she half rises and says "Show me", and when Rosie laughs "Joke", she answers "Not funny. That roll's certified." Her alarm is about her legal audit roll and Trading Standards, which is exactly what Tess would fear. A viewer watching a second time can wonder whether that's all it was.
- **Matching finished episode 1.** Frank's photo is now "a big man in a donkey jacket laughing at whoever held the camera", not a man with his sleeves rolled up. The surgeon in Rosie's videos wears a rig's collar over her gown, and the tumour comes back in her gloved palm. The weigh-in before opening happens with the display dark, and the ticket tells the number. Rosie's 43.1 gets a star on the chalk board like her 43.6, which also matches the "four stars" in the episode 3 draft.
- **Times.** The radio scene is now 06:30 and breakfast 06:45, before the hatch opens at seven. In the radio scene Peg now turns the bacon instead of handing eggs to a driver, because no drivers are inside before seven. Hop two moved from 12:30 to 13:10, after dinner, to fit Tess's meal rule.
- **The painting's last beat.** "Put her away" now comes straight after hop seven, and Rosie posts the painting into the safe while she's still rigged. The draft's line "Nobody in this family has used the key to that safe in years" is cut, because episode 1 already says it ("Nobody's had that door open since Blair").
- **A small slip in the draft.** On Friday "Peg looks at the order book, and the thin envelope in the drawer", but that envelope went to Dilys on Tuesday. She now looks at the order book and the till.
- **"It's his birthday" is now "It's for his birthday"**, both on the phone and at the clinic desk, so nobody wonders why the family isn't having a party on Saturday.
- **The Ahern shed.** The lad now stands on a wooden board hung by chains from the steelyard, rather than sitting in a sling. The plan says "where a skinny lad stands", and episode 10 weighs grown adults on the same steelyard.
- **Two small wording fixes.** "The weave of the linen like a woven field" is now "like a ploughed field" ("woven" twice in one phrase). Peg's "There's nothing left when the black takes you. That's the point of it." is now "...You know that." The old ending didn't mean anything clear. The new one is Peg shutting the subject down.
- **One action line cut.** In the weigh-in, the draft said "She was fourteen when her name first went at the top of that board." The step scene tells us this in Tess's own words two acts later, so the earlier line gave it away.
- **Length.** The episode is about 780 words longer than the draft, and most of that is the rewritten plants list. The new beats (the false alarm, the call on the step, the City cold open) roughly balance the cuts, so the screen time per act is about the same.
