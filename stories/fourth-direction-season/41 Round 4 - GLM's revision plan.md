## Part 2. Changes by episode

### Episode 1
No changes.

### Episode 2
1. Act one, Scene 4 (the weighbridge office, Tuesday, 06:45). After PEG's line ending "…You hold it loose, you let it roll about, and one of these goes it comes back the right way.", and before DANNY's "She's got a face, Mum. She's looking out the window.", insert:

   LIAM: Won't she come back with it? Whichever way it goes?

   PEG: Not if she faces home.

   Everything after stays unchanged, including Danny's joke ("Wrong window") and the laughter.

### Episode 3
No changes.

### Episode 4
No changes.

### Episode 5
No changes.

### Episode 6
1. Cold open, Scene 1. In the setup, after "Seventh night. If the tow runs, the man comes again.", add: "Six nights he has stepped in at four past by the clock and found the black empty. Six crossings, two seconds each, off the plate, and nobody told."
2. Cold open, Scene 1, the glow at the safe. "And through the safe's steel, lit the way everything inside the glow is lit, a white envelope lies on the flat of the painting, let go a moment ago." becomes "And through the safe's steel, lit the way everything inside the glow is lit, a white envelope lies on the flat of the painting, and the fingers that let it go are still sliding back out of the steel." The body stays unnamed; Joe's identification remains where it is, in Scene 2.

### Episode 7
1. Act four, Scene 12 (the stove, one in the morning). In Rosie's speech, "At Mayfair. There was never anybody near me. I never felt a thing." becomes "At Mayfair. There was never anybody near me. A pull in my gut, that's all it ever was." The rest of the speech, on either side, is unchanged.

### Episode 8
1. Act three, Scene 6 (the flat above the bookmaker's). After RAY's "Never touched a penny. It was never mine. It's your uncle's dinners, so it's in your uncle's name, and he never signed anything and owes nobody anything.", add:

   RAY: And you can't touch it either. The audit found the book before it found me. It's an exhibit now, same as the tubs.

   Tess's next line, "Why charge us at all?", follows unchanged.
2. Act four, Scene 7 (the table, eight in the evening). In Tess's account, "The money's in a building society in Grays. In Terry's name. He never spent a penny of it. (beat)" becomes "The money's in a building society in Grays. In Terry's name. He never spent a penny of it. Frozen with the rest. (beat)". The rest of the speech is unchanged.

### Episode 9
No changes.

### Episode 10
1. Act three, Scene 12, "The black — Ray". "The glow opens an arm's length round him, grey-gold, and in it the office stands open like a cutaway: the table's grain, the flue's old soot, and at the end of the plate the green safe — its steel gone clear as water…" becomes "The glow opens an arm's length round him, grey-gold, and in it the office stands open like a cutaway: at the end of the plate the green safe — its steel gone clear as water…". The rest of the sentence (the painting on its tea towel, the light falling from the wrong side) is unchanged.
2. Act three, Scene 12, "The field". "ROSIE: Seven in ten, it's Nan's fry-up for me on Friday. Three in ten, I'm still this." becomes "ROSIE: Seven in ten, it's Nan's fry-up for me. Three in ten, I'm still this."
