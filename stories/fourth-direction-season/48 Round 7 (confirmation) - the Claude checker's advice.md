## CONFIRMED findings

1. **Terry's die mislabels the six (Continuity 1). Touches episodes 9 and 10, and the epilogue.** Ep 9 shows that a turned crosser who turns comes out straight, and ep 10 shows the bite bringing Rosie back from the same shore. So a Terry who turns on the step lands straight and can bite home, on half his 5.7 seconds at about 1 in 5,000, cured. "Six, I stay hungry" is therefore false by the show's own rules, the choice at Mucking loses its real stakes, and the open question of whether Terry arrived straight is weakened, because a straight Terry could have come home. The fix needs no new rule: Terry refuses the bite in his own words (for example, "I'm not biting, love. Whatever hand I land as, I've gone."), so the choice becomes a refusal rather than a roll of the dice.
