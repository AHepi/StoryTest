# Episode 3, "Making Weight": revision log

The draft (`80-episode-03-draft.md`) is left as it was. The revised episode is `82-episode-03-final.md`. I checked every finding in the critique (`81-episode-03-critique.md`) against the master plan (`70-plan-v3.md`) and against the draft. The critique was written before episodes 1 and 2 were finished, so I also checked each point against the finished versions (`82-episode-01-final.md`, `82-episode-02-final.md`). Where they touch this episode, the finished episodes win.

I accepted every finding, in full or in part. The two I took only in part are finding 6 and one of finding 13's five small points (13d). I rejected none, because each one pointed at a real problem. Where I did something different from the critique's fix, the table says what and why. I added no new rule and no new canonical number. None of the plan's banned words reaches the screen, and neither do "lift", "reserve" or "mint". "Spare" appears only in Peg's "a second to spare", meaning time in hand, never the bite.

## Other changes I made, and why

- **Tess no longer says "Nobody can turn anybody."** In the draft she answers Ray's spinner with "Nobody can turn anybody. You face home." But everyone in Grays knows that a handshake in the black turns one of the two people (episode 1's one-handed man on the ferry, "Shook on it, him"). And the plan has Tess herself say so in episode 4. She now says "Not a Marlow. We face home." That is the family's real mistake (facing home does nothing about turning), and it doesn't state the handshake rule an episode early.
- **The tingle now starts at 9.2, not 9.6.** Finished episode 1 settled on the tingle coming about two seconds before a crosser's time runs out. Rosie has 11.2 seconds, so it comes at 9.2, while she is still in the clay. Danny's "Nine four" now follows it. Nothing turns on the exact tenth, but episodes 5 and 6 depend on the audience knowing when the tingle normally comes.
- **The way home.** The draft had Rosie "find the tug of home and follow it up" through the clay, which mixes up two things. Going back up through the ground is ordinary movement. Facing home decides which world you step out onto (the same fix episode 1 made). She now climbs back up the way she came, and faces home as the van's floor comes into her glow.
- **The glucose gel lives in the cutlery drawer** ("In with the meter"), not the "top drawer of the dresser". In episodes 1 and 2 the dresser drawer is where Peg puts unopened letters, and that drawer matters in episodes 4, 9 and 10.
- **Danny buckles on his rig's belt** before posting the tray into the safe. Episode 2 established that anyone who posts something through the safe door wears the belt.
- **Scene 2 now picks up exactly where episode 2 ends.** Rosie is "walking fast" and still saying the van's number plate to herself, not "out of breath".
- **Terry's pot.** Episode 1 has it living in the fridge at night. So in scene 12 Terry takes it out of the fridge before he stands the clean spoon in it, and in scene 8 Rosie walks "past the fridge" to the sink. The spoon standing in the pot, which the critique asked to keep, is kept.
- **Scene 6 shows Tess's reasoning instead of stating it.** She looks along the table at Terry eating alone from his tub, then back at Rosie's clean plate, then picks up her fork.
- **Five more action lines of the same kind as finding 11.** "She has done this for herself in lay-bys and car parks for years" became "quick and sure". "On Peg, in lay-bys, it has always meant sugar, now" is cut. "He counts it in his head, the weeks of fry-ups" is cut, leaving Danny with his face in his hands. "As she has the night before every job since she was fourteen" is cut. "He picked it up off the side at the first LO" became "He has had it in his hands since the first LO."
- **Times.** The new scene 17 is at first light, so the family's table scene moves from 5 a.m. to half past five. In late March, first light comes at about that time.
- **One unclear line.** In the pit scene, "Kieran surfaces. 'Again.'" didn't say who speaks. It is now Tess's line.
- **The plants list is rewritten** to match all of the above. New entries: the three beats that "Till Wednesday" re-reads, the windscreen, Tess's "Not a Marlow. We face home", "It was eleven two", the answer to episode 1's "She can't hear you", and "Paper talks" returning.
- **Length.** About 390 words longer than the draft. Most of that is the new scene 17 and the fuller plants list. The cuts roughly balance the additions, so screen time per act is about the same.
