## Episode 04

**Changes made**

1. **Scene 2, the Saturday-morning war council: Tess's explanation of how Rosie could have been turned (Fix 1).** Her old line was "The copper said there's a way to kill a crosser without leaving a mark. Six months, and it looks like a diet. Turn her round in the black and send her home to her tea — that's the only way it could be done." It is now "The copper said there's a way to kill a crosser without leaving a mark. Sit heavy by her in the black till she loses home. She faces wrong, she comes home wrong, and six months later it looks like a diet." In plain words: the old version had Tess describing a grab-and-turn, which the same scene later proves wouldn't work — grabbing someone in the black turns one of the two crossers at random, so an enemy couldn't use it to turn a chosen victim on purpose. The new version says the enemy simply sat close beside her until she lost her sense of home and stepped out facing the wrong way, which fits the rules, fits Rosie's own story about being "pulled off home", and keeps Ray's "murder that looks like a diet" idea that the war and Declan's mirror-image belief are built on. Everything else in the scene is untouched, including Danny's follow-up escalation to wrenching someone round, the grip-rule exchange that corrects him, and the line "Nobody says anything about any of it."

2. Nothing else in the episode was changed. All scenes, camera directions, dialogue, numbers, names and the Plants and payoffs notes are as they were, because the plan lists only one change for Episode 4. I also checked the fixes that touch other episodes and confirmed they do not reach this one: the "Aye" fix starts at episode 7 (there is no spoken "Aye" in this episode), and the "Nobody" tide naming is deliberately planted in episode 2 and left alone here, as the plan states.

**Planned changes I could not make**

None. The plan lists exactly one change for Episode 4, and it has been made in the ruling's own words.
