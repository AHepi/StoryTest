# SECONDS: revision log, round 1 (plan version 1 to version 2)

**What this is.** Three critics read plan version 1 (`30-plan-v1.md`): one on the laws and physics, one on craft (care, suspense, structure), one on theme and originality. Every finding was checked against the plan, the brief, your second feedback and The Fourth Direction itself. Most were right. A few were right about the problem but offered a fix that would have caused a new one, so I used a different fix and say why. None was rejected outright. Version 2 is `50-plan-v2.md`; version 1 is kept unchanged.

**Verdicts used.** *Accepted:* the problem is real and the suggested fix went in. *Accepted in part:* the problem is real, but part of the fix was changed or left out, for the reason given. *Rejected:* the finding is wrong or would make the plan worse (none this round).

## 4. Changes made to keep the plan coherent

These weren't asked for by any critic. Each was needed so the accepted fixes fit together.

- **Six guesses, not five.** Section 5 now says six and bans a seventh. The spare is revised in place; the grip is new.
- **Two scales at the weighbridge.** Section 3.1 now introduces the little bridge (people, sacks, the test weight, ten-gram steps) beside the big bridge (lorries, twenty-kilo steps). Every weigh-in moved to the little bridge; both print on the audit roll, so Kieran's nightly tickets are part of the evidence.
- **Where the rigs are.** Ray takes Rosie's rig in ep 5, so in ep 6 Tess crosses because Rosie has none and Danny is maimed. Peg locks every rig away in ep 7 and takes them out for the raid in ep 9, leaving only Tess's old rig on the rack, which is the one Rosie uses in eps 9 and 10.
- **Ep 3's ten seconds** now come from both the one-trip rule and Ray's stakeout: Tess's plan was two short trips from the basement next door; the rule forbids it, and Ray's car outside the basement forces the long way round.
- **Why Rosie walks out on raid night.** A text from Kieran ("Nan's doing the depot tonight. They won't let me go.") joins her walkout to the raid, so Kieran is alone in the office when she lands.
- **Why Tess is home in ep 9.** The ward rings her as next of kin when Rosie goes missing.
- **Ray's "bill".** The family paid the Laundry; Ray charged the black-market price so Peg wouldn't smell charity, and the money sits untouched in an account in Terry's name.
- **Peg's receipt and key.** The framed Ministry receipt ("found wet, sand adhering") is background from ep 1 and one of the three records that find the door. Peg's old key opening the old gate pays her hidden want, her field back.
- **Peg's "turned"** moved from the fence to the night of writing, so the fence has no farewell.
- **Shaun's legs** are both eight seconds (with the cargo counted, nine seconds was beyond his real budget); one comes back straight, one turned.
- **Terry's odds** now depend on how long he takes: almost nothing at three seconds, one in six at the full five. Rosie's crossings are two or three seconds, so they slide.
- **Ep 10's investigators** needed one check: the raid-night tickets, corrected by the April letter and with the cases added, match the depot walls, so handing over the rolls really does convict.
- **Names and titles.** Ray's unit is the Met Crossing Unit. Maggie's waterman words no longer include "slack".
- **Every table updated to match:** the ledger (all rows re-checked for episode and job), the twist schedule, the knowledge map (new rows for Con, the grip and Kieran's hunger), the wonders (Frank's "held still" removed), the ending, the physics rules (now twenty), the writers' notes and the canonical numbers.
- **Section 15** now says which entries are new in version 2, declares the flu and the likeness to pitch B's ending, and says what was removed from pitch C's material.

## 5. Still unresolved

1. **A sixth guess.** The grip's price is new physics, declared once. Without it the free-cure hole stays open, and I found no other fix that closes it without also breaking Danny's grab. You may still prefer fewer guesses.
2. **The Hill's pull on Grays.** The Hill is about eight kilometres from Grays; a change there would mostly pull sideways, so a 1.1 per cent drop in weight at Grays is generous even with the book's dial turned up. No critic raised it. If you want it tighter, the change could be put in the neighbours' sea directly under Grays instead, at the cost of reworking ep 4's "The Hill's moved".
3. **Dark-matter maps** have only a small teaching job. It is honest, but it is the thinnest row in the ledger.
4. **The ep 6 stakeout** is still an instrument showing an unseen visitor at night. The craft critic allowed it as a stakeout. If it still feels close to The Catch's needle, Danny can simply wait by the safe with no readout.
5. **Terry's open fate** also resembles Arthur C. Clarke's story "Technical Error" (a mirror-reversed man sent back through and lost). Terry goes to be fed, not to be turned back, and he chose it, but the likeness is worth knowing.
6. **Ep 10 is still full:** Frank, the night of writing, two crossings, the investigators and the rolls. The intercut helps; a table read may show it needs a few extra minutes or a lighter act two.
7. **Handshakes** invite the question why more turned people don't try them. The plan answers it in one line (free food against a hand on a coin toss) and on screen through Danny and Terry. A season two would have to face it.
8. **Mirror food after the epilogue.** Once the odds are public, anyone could make mirror food by carrying food on long crossings. The plan leaves this to season two.
9. **Terry stripped and washed at the fence** is right by the physics but a tone risk; it needs restraint in the writing.
