# Revision log: `30-season-plan.md` → `50-season-plan-revised.md`

Every finding in the three critiques was checked against the plan (and, where it quoted or paraphrased them, against the two source files and the owner's theories). Each entry says whether it was accepted and what changed in the revised plan, by section or episode. None was wrong outright, so none is rejected in full. Six had a sub-point that was wrong or clashed with another accepted fix; those sub-points are rejected and the reason is given.

**Where two critiques asked for different fixes, this is how they were settled:**
- Logic 6b (make Kit's silence visible) against Craft 1b (keep Kit's count as the final surprise). Both are kept. Kit argues openly in episode 9 against warning Ream. The audience sees him hide a book at the Mother's needle in episode 8. Only in the last scene do we learn it is thirty years of counts.
- Logic 6a (Kit never built a channel) against Theme 15b (explain the spelled-name lore). Kit did use the channel once, spelling CARROLL in about Year 254, and Ream Joined his great-aunt. That is why he stopped, and it is where the lore comes from.
- Theme 6 / Craft 10 (where the pair test goes). It moves to episode 7, act 2. Episode 6's maintenance hour ends with the port cut, and that episode is already full.
- Logic 2 and Theme 8 (what turns as one piece). These are merged into one declared stipulation.

## Changes added to keep the plan coherent (not asked for by any critique)

- A pair is closed on the heavier crosser's count. This was needed once the ring rule (L1) and Wells's shorter count (L12) were both in.
- Stipulation 2 now says that with long enough beside the Weight, more than half turn. This declares the 62 (T2) instead of slipping it in.
- Four turned plates ("the plates that face home"). They follow from stipulation 7, are shown from ep 1 as surplus, and are named in the Litany (ep 9).
- WELLS. STARVING. replaces "seven single pulses" in the ep 8 hook. The old version clashed with Kit's seven, and both new words are in the Leaves.
- The Randall takes the MOVE THE DOOR sheet off the drum herself, her first act in Nell's favour.
- Wells's argument for sending: "Nell won't obey it. Nell will ask why." That is why a message is safe now when CARROLL wasn't.
- Theta carries a needle to the Graves and says "Arrived". Hester straps stones to Nell as well.
- The twins are in "this day's last six pairs", so that three Turned on the day fits a rate of 24 in 100.
- The ep 9 ballast reasoning no longer assumes Nell knows about Minthome's pile.
- Nan's tale says "crossed eleven times", to match Kit's "seven" correction.

## Still unresolved

- Episode 9 is still the densest episode: a cold open, the 62, the board, the Litany, *Day One* and the heist. If a draft overruns, move Pim's scene with the Randall to the end of episode 8.
- The grand total "about 7 in 100" was kept, not recomputed. It is plausible only if Ream's population grew a lot, so recent years weigh most. Eight tonnes a year from Year 131 also implies Ream already had several thousand people by then. Neither is stated in the plan.
- Three physical beats are asserted, not derived: the needle rising, holding and falling for an arrival *into* Ream; a hand "set down inside rock"; and a crosser whose count is too short "falling short of the world". They are declared as derived lines but held loosely.
- "The mint alone is witness" was carved by a past hidden Turned Randall to protect the lineage. It sustains the lie, not the doom, but it is a small secret act at a pivot. It should be rechecked against the rule of no secret villain.
- The critiques have not been rerun on the revision. A fresh logic and theme pass is recommended before episode drafting.
