## Episode 01

1. **Scene 3, the breakfast table after the dip (finding 2).** Liam's line now ends "Half the beads" instead of "Wall never floods". The reason: the old wording implied each of his two quick trips would be short enough to slip under the weigh-wall's three-second flood, but a trip that short could never have reached the Mayfair shelf, roughly five seconds in, so the arithmetic didn't hold. "Half the beads" keeps his argument — two trips, less bead poured down each time — without the impossible timing. As the plan orders, the family's chorus reply ("Never go back twice") is untouched, and so is everything around the line. Episode 6's echo of this moment is changed separately by its own entry in the plan; episode 3's "That's what I said at Mayfair" still lands, because Liam still proposes going in twice.

2. **Plants and payoffs, the Terry entry (finding 7, documentation only).** "He is never weighed" now reads "He is never weighed on screen this season, until the night of writing in 10", because episode 10 now contains the wordless beat where Terry steps on the little bridge and Kieran tears off his 70.6 ticket. Without this edit the entry would contradict the new scene.

3. **Plants and payoffs, the Kieran entry (finding 9, documentation only).** I added "; she names the week to Kieran in 10" after "(her wound)", pointing forward to the new exchange at the night of writing where Tess tells Kieran the different week was the week Frank died. Scene 7 itself is unchanged, as the plan keeps it as the plant.

All three changes the plan lists for Episode 1 were made. None could not be made. Nothing else in the episode was altered, and I checked that no other line, plant note or number here depends on the old wording.

## Episode 07

Changes I made:

1. **The envelope in Tess's car (finding 3, scene 3).** Originally Tess opened the white envelope with her thumbnail, one clean slit, on the very morning the note was dated, which clashed with her saying later "Two weeks I've had that in my pocket." Now the envelope is already slit — she slit it days after it came — and she takes the strip of paper out and reads it again. I also cut the line "She reads it twice.", because the new wording ("reads it again") already covers the second reading and the plan asked for it to go.

2. **"Bar Danny, and that was the grip, not the dice." (finding 8).** The plan asks for Tess to add this sentence right after "Not one, either side of the river, not in twenty-five years", because Danny turned at 2.2 seconds and the claim would otherwise leave him unaccounted for. One small note: the plan calls this moment "scene 9", but in the episode as written that exact sentence sits in the intercut ferry scene (scene 11), where the crowded sheet is read out — it appears nowhere else in this form — so I made the insertion there, at the identified words. Scene 12's shorter repeat at the family table ("Not one under three seconds, either side, not in twenty-five years") was left exactly as it was, as the plan orders, and now plays as already qualified because the audience heard the caveat first.

3. **The plants and payoffs entry for the envelope (finding 3, documentation only).** I rewrote the entry to match the new moment: the envelope was slit open days after it came and carried a fortnight; Tess's "Two weeks I've had that in my pocket" (scene 6) is true of the knowing, not the opening; she reads it again in the car on the day, which she says aloud on the river road. The rest of the entry (the ferry at Nobody low, "just you", the note dated Thursday the 21st) is unchanged.

4. **The finding 2 check.** As the plan records, I verified there is no repeat of "under three" as the safer-split idea anywhere in this episode, so nothing needed changing for that finding. The wall's flood-count lines ("The wall floods at three", "The wall will go at three. Let it.") are untouched, as ordered.

Nothing in the plan for Episode 7 was impossible to make: all three listed changes were made. Everything else in the episode — every scene, line, number, plant and payoff — is unchanged, so nothing breaks in the other episodes (episode 6 still shows Tess pocketing the note unopened; her "two weeks" line still lands; episode 9's payoffs and the season's canonical numbers all still stand).
