## Fix (11 findings)

1. **Episode 5, scene 13: the tab's field works in the sea, and nobody says why.** Fable 1, and it follows from my own round-1 fix, which planted "Water counts" in episode 1.
   - As Tess tugs Liam's tab, after "Only once you've stood a minute on the wrong side.", add one line, for example: "Tab's field's skin-tight. You and what's on you, nothing else. It'll fire in water. Your belt won't."
2. **Episode 4, scene 2: why Danny can't grab Joe with a hand sent across, which never turns.** Fable 2. The plan has the rule ("A hand sent from a body still on the leaf can't grip anyone in the black"), but nobody says it.
   - After "Collar does nothing. It's skin, or it's nothing.", Tess adds one line, for example: "And don't think you'll do it with a posted hand. A hand still stood on the world can't hold anything that's off it. Slides off him like smoke."
3. **Episode 10, scene 6: the Register's investigators arrive with no stated reason.** Fable 3, and Fable's quibble 1 in round 1. Give the investigator one more line before "We'd like the family's weights", for example: "Your granddaughter walked off our Whitechapel ward at eleven that night, Mrs Marlow, and her next of kin is this address."
4. **Episode 6, cold open: the stakeout's first night is the night Joe comes.** Fable 4, story critic in round 1, MiMo's critique this round. Change "If the tow runs tonight, the man comes again." to "Seventh night. If the tow runs, the man comes again."
5. **Episodes 8 and 9: the fortnight of hunger costs nothing on screen, and Peg gives no reason for the date.** Fable 5.
   - *Episode 8, scene 7:* after "Wednesday fortnight.", Peg adds a reason, for example "That's when the Army's lot goes in. Shed's full then."
   - *Episode 9, scene 1:* one wordless beat of cost. Danny punches a new hole in his belt with a nail, the way Liam did in episode 5.
6. **Episode 10: Rosie's second bite home is a toss the finale forgets, and "nobody has brought one" contradicts Peg's meter, which "she brings everywhere now".** Fable 6.
   - Change "and nobody has brought one" to "and nobody reaches for one".

   - Do not have her read a sign or any print to know it. The plan bans that as an echo of *The Catch*.
7. **Episode 7: Joe "timing your girl" through a 9.4-second crossing is past his own budget.** MiMo 1, confirmed. At 59.0 kilos the rule gives him 8.2 seconds. Maggie's line becomes "Sat in the dark off your shelf with a stopwatch, timing what he could of your girl, so we'd know what a Marlow's made of."
8. **Episode 10: Danny never owns his "Yes" to Liam, after demanding "Did your sums do Liam too?"** MiMo 3, confirmed; the people critic raised it in round 1. At the night of writing, near Rosie's line about Liam, Danny adds one line, for example: "And I said yes. Write that first."
9. **Episode 8: Ray's spinner is never closed with him.** MiMo 4, confirmed. It came back from round 2, where I left it. Episode 8's own notes promise a line the scene does not contain: "There's no such person. There never was." Put that line into the flat scene, said to Ray by Tess or by Ray himself, where it fits the talk of files.
10. **Episodes 7 and 10: the pencil that "never once come down" came down in episode 3 for Rosie's writing test.** MiMo 5, confirmed.
    - In episode 7, change "and never once come down" to "and come down once in all that time, for Rosie's name".

    - "It has never been sharpened" stays.
11. **Episode 8: Ray's ledger doubles on 7 April, three days after the family knew, yet he says "I knew there was another mouth in that house before you did".** MiMo 6, confirmed. Change his line to "I knew there was another mouth in that house the week you did."
