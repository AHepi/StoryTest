## Part 2 — changes by episode

### Episode 1
No changes.

### Episode 2
No changes.

### Episode 3
No changes.

### Episode 4
1. **Scene 2 (the war council, Saturday 06:30).** Tess's speech "Collar does nothing. It's skin, or it's nothing." gains the ruled line, appended to the same speech:
   > TESS: Collar does nothing. It's skin, or it's nothing. And don't think you'll do it with a posted hand. A hand still stood on the world can't hold anything that's off it. Slides off him like smoke.
   Rosie's line from the stairs ("And whoever turns leaves the hand in there.") follows unchanged.

### Episode 5
1. **Scene 13 (the weigh-in, Wednesday 4:30 a.m.).** Tess's speech about the bite becomes:
   > TESS: Not in the black. Only once you've stood a minute on the wrong side. *(beat)* Tab's field's skin-tight. You and what's on you, nothing else. It'll fire in water. Your belt won't. *(beat)* And step out on purpose, it's twenty years.
   Liam's "Liam nods, as if he'd always known." and everything after stay unchanged.

### Episode 6
1. **Scene 1 (cold open).** In the narration, "If the tow runs tonight, the man comes again." becomes "Seventh night. If the tow runs, the man comes again." Nothing else in the scene moves, including Danny's "Wait for the printer" line and the 02:04 timing.

### Episode 7
1. **Scene 7 (the ferry saloon).** Maggie's line becomes: "Mine was the second. Seventeen year old, and I sent him. Sat in the dark off your shelf with a stopwatch, timing what he could of your girl, so we'd know what a Marlow's made of."
2. **Scene 9 (the graph).** "Tess's hand goes up behind her ear, to the pencil that has lived there for twenty years and never once come down." becomes "…to the pencil that has lived there for twenty years and come down once in all that time, for Rosie's name." The next sentence, "She holds it a second. It has never been sharpened.", stays.

### Episode 8
1. **Scene 6 (the flat), the ledger.** "Nobody doubles an order like that for a guest. I knew there was another mouth in that house before you did." becomes "Nobody doubles an order like that for a guest. I knew there was another mouth in that house the week you did."
2. **Scene 6 (the flat), the talk of files.** After "TESS: Of what?" and "He has no answer. He squares the files instead, edges to edges, and looks at them.", insert:
   > TESS: You told me once there was a way to kill a crosser that takes six months and looks like a diet.
   > RAY: There's no such person. There never was.
   "TESS: What happens now?" follows unchanged.
3. **Scene 7 (the vote on the raid).** Peg's speech becomes: "Long crossings, then. Wednesday fortnight. That's when the Army's lot goes in. Shed's full then. We'll feed our own."

### Episode 9
1. **Scene 1 (the office, raid night, ten o'clock).** One wordless beat of cost, placed after "…because no power on earth would have kept him upstairs." and before "SHAUN (26, on a stick since the leg) steps onto the little bridge…":
   > At the table's end, Danny sits with a nail from the toolbox, working a new hole through his belt — rough at the edges, the way his son's was — and pulls the strap in a notch tighter. Nobody says anything.

### Episode 10
1. **Scene 6 (the investigators, noon).** After "She looks at the bare board, and at her clipboard, and back.", the investigator's speech begins with the ruled line, the existing sentence unchanged after it:
   > INVESTIGATOR: Your granddaughter walked off our Whitechapel ward at eleven that night, Mrs Marlow, and her next of kin is this address. *(beat)* We'd like the family's weights, Mrs Marlow. To match.
2. **Scene 7 (the night of writing).** After ROSIE's "And write my lie down. Something took hold of me. That's Con.", insert:
   > DANNY: And I said yes. Write that first.
   "No reply. Kieran writes it, and the night goes on." stays, and now covers both lines.
3. **Scene 9 (four in the morning, the baking tray).** "— the one that has lived there for twenty years and never come down for anyone —" becomes "— the one that has lived there for twenty years and come down once, for Rosie —".
4. **Scene 12 (05:52, the field), Rosie's return.** After "Wet grass, fog, the gate, her family.", add her first words, settling her own toss at once, with no test of any kind and nothing read:
   > ROSIE: Still me.
   DANNY's "What did you see?" follows unchanged.
5. **Scene 12, Terry's going.** "…and nobody looks at any instrument, because there isn't one, and nobody has brought one, and that was the choice they made in the kitchen at four in the morning." becomes "…and nobody looks at any instrument, because there isn't one, and nobody reaches for one, and that was the choice they made in the kitchen at four in the morning."
