# 21 Check - my own reading of *Seconds* version 3

Before any episode is written, I checked version 3 of the plan myself instead of taking its own word that it passes. Here is what I checked and what I found.

## What I checked, and how

- **Your rules, by searching the whole plan for words and echoes.**
  - The banned words (conjecture, refute, falsify, fallible, hard to vary, growth of knowledge, static or dynamic society, "nothing is settled", "question everything", infinity) and any sign of the theory of explanation (semantics, Popper, Deutsch, Fable). Result: the only match is in the writers' list of words that must never reach the screen.
  - Echoes of *The Catch*: mint, wristband, dishes, flask, oxygen, cylinder, a reserve bar, hands on glass, a lift shaft or cage, and the names Nell, Iona, Jude, Eli and Saye. Result: none. The only "dishes" are satellite dishes on a school poster.
  - Words that would signal a dystopia (regime, curfew, police state, camp). Result: none.
- **The ten episodes, by reading them in full.** They hold together:
  - The family's own vault concrete makes the long jobs that turn people.
  - A scale that has been lying since the neighbours dug under Grays, and one fry-up, kill Liam by two hundredths of a second.
  - Rosie's forbidden step finds a sea that is food for the turned.
  - The family's founding legend, "Grandad was too heavy", turns out to have been a turned man starving in secret.
  - The field where the object arrived turns out to be a shore of the neighbours' sea at the lowest tide.
  - The ending is a vote to hand the records over rather than burn them.
- **The revision logs' "Still unresolved" sections**, read in full.

## What I did not check

- I did not check every number in the plan's number table by hand. The round 2 laws critic and the reviser both did; I spot-read the episode sums for Liam and Frank, and they match.
- I did not read every row of the law ledger. The round 2 laws critic ran the full checklist; the reviser re-ran it and reports every item present and working.

## Ten choices the plan made that you may want to overrule

The plan has a working default for each, so the episodes go ahead on the defaults. Tell me if you want any changed.

1. **Holding hands in the dark.** A grip turns one of the pair, so two turned people could cure one of themselves this way. The price is the hand that held on. Every cure is still another crossing and another coin toss nobody can feel.
2. **A worldwide ripple.** One line treats a sudden, violent event next door as a ripple felt everywhere, because the book's own examples need it.
3. **The first "letter".** The neighbours' message begins with the dig that made the scale lie. So their invitation is one of three causes of Liam's death.
4. **The minus ticket.** A scale pulled from above prints a minus. It is a technical beat.
5. **Rosie's minute next door.** It needs careful staging.
6. **Episode 1** carries many single lines, and may need a trim.
7. **Episode 10** is still full.
8. **Cheap mirror food,** once the drift is public, is left for a second season.
9. **Terry washed at the standpipe:** backs to camera, no words.
10. **The door is timed by the neighbours' tide.** That idea is borrowed from the third pitch, in this one place only, and declared as borrowed.
