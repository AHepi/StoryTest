1. **Scene 18 (the Obverse clinic, Saturday, 09:55).** Between the receptionist's "Take a seat. Dr Osei won't be long." and the existing final line, insert:

   > ROSIE: Where's your toilet?
   >
   > RECEPTIONIST: Down the stairs, under them.
   >
   > Rosie goes down: a corridor, a consulting-room door, stairs to the lower ground, the toilet under the stairs. Past the toilet stands a steel door: RECORDS. STAFF ONLY. A keypad beside the handle. She passes it at an ordinary walk, and comes back up, and sits on the edge of a leather chair with her hands between her knees.

   Two seconds of screen time. No linger, no reaction beyond the walk. The corridor, stairs, toilet-under-the-stairs and door wording match Rosie's recount in episode 6 exactly.

### Episode 8

1. **New Scene 5A (the weighbridge office, Grays, Tuesday, mid-morning).** Inserted between scenes 5 and 6, numbered 5A so no scene is renumbered:

   > The hatch is up; the lorries run. MICK leans in for his ticket and his bacon roll.
   >
   > MICK: Purfleet's shut to us Wednesday fortnight, Peg. Army's in. Whole shed.
   >
   > PEG: You'll live.
   >

   Mick's line is the ruling's wording verbatim. This is the ruling's "give it to the hatch, where lorry drivers eat every morning": scene 7's evening meeting has the hatch down and the gate shut, so the morning beat carries the knowledge, the same day Peg uses it.

Wednesday fortnight. Army's in, like Mick says. Shed's full then. We'll feed our own." Nothing else in the scene changes.
3. **Plants and payoffs:** amend the "**The depot recon (9):**" bullet to name the source of the day: "…gatehouse, two cameras, a lorry every ten minutes, cases on pallets — and the day itself off Mick at the hatch (Scene 5A), which is why Peg's chalk says Wednesday fortnight, when the Army's consignment fills the shed."
