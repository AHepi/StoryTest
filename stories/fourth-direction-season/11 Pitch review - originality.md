# Originality review of the three season pitches

**The lens.** Originality only. For each pitch this review lists what it resembles (The Catch, the rejected Caraway, and well-known films, series and books), says how close each one is and whether the pitch makes it its own, checks the religion rule, then gives strengths, serious problems with fixes, and a verdict.

**What I compared against.** The brief's lists; the owner's notes on The Catch (the short answer, what works, faults 1 to 4); Caraway's section 0, its world section (1.1 and 1.2) and its episode titles; and The Fourth Direction itself. Anything taken straight from The Fourth Direction is allowed and wanted, so a source item is flagged only when The Catch or Caraway already used it the same way.

**How close:**
- **Copy:** the same device doing the same job.
- **Close:** the same shape in different clothes. The owner would likely notice.
- **Echo:** a viewer who knows the other work would notice.
- **Faint:** only a specialist would notice. No action needed.

---

## What all three share

Read side by side, the three pitches converge. The owner may read all three, so this matters.

- **The same source items.** All three use the grip that turns one of two hand-holders, the neighbours' sea under the town, a mid-season earthquake with no fault that shifts the world, a lost crosser's weight lingering in an empty place, the dog, jar lids, the held breath, the amputee's extra seconds and blindfold training in water. Each is fine on its own. But when all three reach for the same eight or nine lines, the source starts to look small. Each pitch should own at least one change in the world that the other two don't have.
- **The obvious ending.** Two of the three (B and C) end with a turned person going to live next door, where the food fits. The source calls this the ending that "writes itself", and Caraway used it (Minthome). A tests the same hope and it fails, which is the fresher move.
- **The house ending.** All three end with a community weighing or counting in public: A's rolls, B's fair and Saturday weighing, C's chalk board. Caraway also ended with children at the Tally board. A does it most quietly, through a cost (handing the records to the police). C does it most like Caraway.
- **Titles collide.** "Slack Water" is C's title and A's episode 7. "No Fault" is B's episode 7 and C's episode 6.

Nothing in A echoes The Catch's lift cage, kind figure, ship, child's drawing, toy carriage, needle, wristband, germ dishes, flask, oxygen, reserve bar, ledge or hands on glass. Nothing echoes Caraway's walled city, rite at seventeen, twins, scripture, heretics, hauled stone or moving the machine.

### Religion rule

Religion is left out, which the rule allows. "Straight?" and "Face home" work like family superstitions, and the one new custom, standing a minute beside a lost crosser's weight, is secular and could only exist in this world. Nothing stock. The owner called the religious angle "fine", and A loses nothing by dropping it.

### Strengths

- **The scale lies because it is honest.** The neighbours move a mountain, and the extra pull makes the weighbridge read light while the rig counts true mass. Liam is sent with a tenth of a second too little. This turns "the only honest channel is weight" into the thing that kills, and I know of no film that has done it. It is planted fairly (the lorry driver, the binned envelope), and no other twist could replace it.
- **The founding legend turned in the dark.** "Fifteen stone" was Peg's memory. The tickets show a thin, starving, turned Frank, and the sum was on screen in episode 1 (95 kilos buys one second, too few for any job). New, and cruel in the right way.
- **The painting proves the carrier.** "If the painting turned, so did whoever carried it." A detective's inference built from the source's forgery item.
- **The family's legal trade lengthens its crimes.** Much of the City's gold sits in Marlow concrete, and those defences are why jobs grew from two seconds to nine. This is the most original irony in A, and it is buried in one line.
- **Exile tested, and it fails.** Rosie follows the Deep to "where the tea is" and finds the floor of a sea that doesn't know she exists. It answers the source's obvious ending, and Caraway's, where B and C walk straight into it.
- **Paper.** A family whose first rule is "nothing on paper" is undone, and saved, by 24 years of bridge tickets the law made them keep.
- **Texture nobody else has:** Essex gravel, a public weighbridge, weigh-walls that flood the goods with tungsten beads, HAND: C on a passport, the tabloid headline "COME ROUND FOR TEA".

### Serious problems and fixes

1. **The engine of the doom is Caraway's Fast.** In Caraway, admired fasting buys a longer count, and the longer count turns you. In A, admired "making weight" buys seconds, "the lightest goes longest", and long jobs turn people. An owner who read Caraway will see it.
   *Fix:* put the cause on two things A has and Caraway never had.
   - **The concrete.** Say it on screen, early: Danny pours a vault floor by day that the family will rob by night, and every year's pour adds a second to the job.
   - **A pride native to crime.** For example, "never go back twice": a second visit used to get thieves caught, so a Marlow takes the whole haul in one trip. Since the odds of turning grow with time in the black, one nine-second trip turns far more people than three short ones. The rule was right once, it is admired, and it now kills.
   - Keep "making weight" and the applause, but as the thing that hides Rosie's starvation, not the thing that causes the turning.
2. **"The Chemist" names Breaking Bad.** *Fix:* call the supplier by what the family sees: "the laundry", "Dilys's van", "the milkman". Keep the reveal.
3. **The crime-family shell is familiar.** The matriarch at the fry-up, the boy who wants in, the code, the rival family, the cop: that is Animal Kingdom and a dozen British gangland dramas. *Fix:* lean harder on what no crime family has had. Peg keeps a public, certified weighbridge: the town's honest scale is kept by thieves. Make the weigh-in the family's ritual and the tickets its memory. Trim the fry-up beats that could sit in any gangster show.
4. **Borrowed words and names.** *Fix:* invent a jewellers' quarter instead of Hatton Garden. Swap "lift" for a family word ("a hop", "a dip", "a go"). Give Kieran a want of his own (to cross) and let his one good question come out of it, rather than being his label.

### Verdict

The most original of the three against The Catch and Caraway, and the only one that answers the source's obvious ending instead of using it. Its best reveals (the honest scale that lies, the turned memory, the painting) are native to the physics and could only happen in this world. Its one real borrowing, admired thinness that buys the seconds that turn you, is Caraway's, and the cure is already in the pitch: the concrete. Rename the Chemist and it is ready to take forward.

## Pitch B: HONEST WEIGHT

### What it resembles

| Element in B | Resembles | How close | Made its own? |
|---|---|---|---|
| A prairie farm family; a blight (rust) kills every crop but one; a dust-dark 1935 frame with the heroine grown; a "ghost" that speaks through gravity by making a clock skip; the family thinks it is the lost man | Interstellar: a blight that leaves only one crop; Dust Bowl interviews with the old; the bedroom "ghost" that turns out to be Cooper, sending a message by gravity through the second hand of his daughter's watch | Close | Partly. B's ghost is a stranger, and the stumble is a lovely reason. But dust, blight, one crop, a farm family, a clock, gravity and a grown daughter remembering add up to Interstellar. The Fourth Direction's own preface names Nolan, so the owner will be primed to see it |
| Nan, the revered elder, secretly turned for thirty years, eats apart, and her tongue is the only test ("Nan's mouth is never wrong") | Caraway's Randall: the high keeper, secretly turned, eating alone; and the carved rule "The mint alone is witness", which makes taste the only test | Close | Partly. | Nan goes next door, where the food fits | The source's "exile is a cure ... the ending writes itself"; Caraway's Passing to Minthome | Close | Partly. The dance and the listening stranger are new; the destination is the stock one |
| The neighbours raise a floor for one old woman; "far off, lanterns, waiting" | The Catch's kind figure who takes people onto a ship and tends them | Echo | Partly. They never appear, but they are still kind hosts receiving a human |
| A devout rural community starving its children, a "living saint", a medical observer, hidden eating | The Wonder (Emma Donoghue's 2016 novel and the 2022 film) | Echo | Yes in mechanism (bread, not a fast); no in the saint trappings |
| A doctor finds the town's pride is the poison; the council replaces him and doubles the dose; the mayor is family | Ibsen's An Enemy of the People | Echo | Yes. The ending is a public weighing done by the people, not a doctor's speech |

| Nan "taken into God's dark palm" and "sent back to feed people"; the saint who carries a dead child out of the diphtheria ward, singing, untouched | The near-death story of being sent back with a mission; the film Resurrection (1980); plague-saint legends | Echo | Partly. Her vision ("it's all lamps inside") is the physics, and fresh. The mission and the saint are stock |
| Tam, the stranger who rides in with a dangerous skill, uses it for others and cannot stay | Shane | Echo | Declared as a guest genre; fine if kept light |
| Clocks stopped at twenty to three; a woman stops a clock and weeps | Miss Havisham's stopped clocks in Great Expectations | Faint | Yes. Here the clocks stop because a pendulum is a gravity meter |
| A blighted farm, a pious family, wasting children | The Witch (2015) | Faint | Yes |

Nothing echoes The Catch's lift, trio of wife, husband and brother, child's drawing, toy, needle, wristband, dishes, flask, oxygen, reserve or glass. Nothing echoes Caraway's city, rite, twins, scripture, heretics or stone.

### Religion rule

Half passes. The fresh half: a woman's private faith grew from something real she saw (every inside lit like a lamp), and her faith's charity is what spreads the famine. That is faith shown in a way the audience hasn't seen. The stock half: the town calling her a saint, the miracle of the untouched nurse in the plague, "sent back" with a mission, and a doctor weighing souls. That is the familiar kit of the sceptic's reveal.

### Strengths

- **The bread that fills and doesn't feed.** Turned wheat that no rust, weevil or mould can touch, because nothing on Earth can eat it, people included. The source's weapon (mirror an enemy's seed vault) becomes a gift, a fortune and a charity. The single best invention in the three pitches.
- **The leaven.** Mirror life spread into every kitchen by generosity, because nothing else will raise the flour. New.
- **Real instruments of 1911 doing the work:** the polarimeter that reads which way a sugar turns light; a tintype (which reverses by nature) matching a modern print mole for mole; the Dominion Cerealist judging wheat by chewing it; pendulum clocks that stop when gravity twitches. Each is true to the period and each carries a clue.
- **"Chosen by mouth since 1790."** The seed catalogue invents the tradition. Advertising copy as the town's fossil wisdom is a fresh angle on an old idea.
- **The Hnatiuks** are saved by their own unexamined rule, by luck, so the show doesn't teach "outsiders are wise".
- **"Nobody fakes a stumble."** The dancer underneath has learned Nan's waltz, stumble and all.
- **Andrew's glove tally on the granary beam,** "6 in 20": the man called a deserter was counting.

### Serious problems and fixes

1. **Interstellar's shell.** *Fix:* cut the 1935 Dust Bowl frame, or move it to a year without dust and without a clock. Move the waltz off the kitchen clock and onto B's own instruments: the grain elevator's scale beam dipping in three-four at nine, or Pryce's balance. Keep the 1909 night when every pendulum clock in town stopped. That is the source's gravimeter twitch, and it is a single event, not a message.
2. **Caraway's secretly turned elder whose taste is the only test.** *Fix:* take the tasting out of the doom. The pitch already has a better engine: "save only the cleanest ears". In a rust year the only clean ears in the district are the turned ring round the Stone, so the rule picks the poison by itself, with no mouth involved. Then either make Nan's turn known family history from 1882 (the family's pride is feeding her from that ring), or keep it secret but let it matter for something other than choosing the seed. The town's blind spot becomes good husbandry and charity, not one saint.
3. **The exile ending.** B's finale is the ending the source calls obvious and the one Caraway used. *Fix:* keep Nan here. The town that burns Mercy fences one acre of it by the Stone, for her alone, weighed and labelled: the first exception the town makes knowing exactly why. The dancer underneath still answers, and she dances back from our side. Or let her try to go and find the floor was never meant for her. Either costs more, and neither has been done.
4. **The saint kit.** *Fix:* drop "saint", the plague miracle and the soul-weighing. Let the town's trust in Nan be practical: the best farmer, the midwife who never lost a mother. Give Pryce a plainer want (why did the hens stop laying?). Retitle episode 2.
5. **The last line that says the message.** *Fix:* end the 1935 tag on the women trying the new seed on the hens and the scale, with no child asking why.

### Verdict

The richest raw material of the three. The bread, the leaven and the period instruments are the most original ideas on the table. But as pitched it wears Interstellar's clothes, hides a Caraway reveal at its centre, and ends where Caraway ended. All three can be fixed without losing the bread. Fixed, it could be the most distinctive show of the three. Unfixed, the owner will probably recognise two of its bones.

### Religion rule

Fails in spirit. The pitch says "no religion", but it builds a healing shrine: pilgrims, a living saint whose sleeve they touch (the Gospel's hem of the garment), a sacred Hand, a miracle "audited" the way Lourdes audits cures, a founder's key, a memorial, and sayings like "The Tide took him" and "We don't speak of it". That is the stock form of religion with the name taken off, which is what the owner objected to.

### Strengths

- **The diving belt.** The founder made the first crossing in her old weight belt, out of habit, so the ferryman was always the heavier one. The reason the cure worked hangs by the Pool in a photograph everyone walks past. Clean, physical, fairly planted.
- **The Strengthening.** The town's kindest act, feeding the pilgrims before they cross, is what flips the cure onto its own sons. The doom grows from kindness, just as the brief asks.
- **A tide that matches nothing in the sky.** Loch Time on the clock tower, a calendar that slides two hours a day, weddings on the flood. Wonderful, lived-in texture.
- **Jess sends jars across alone and counts.** The source's "send a hand, not a body" turned into a test anyone could run.
- **A cloud of the dead can only spread; this one gains weight.** A strong, fair clue.

### Serious problems and fixes

1. **It is Caraway again.** Mint jars and the word "caraway"; a sacred rule about holding hands; mass nearby tilting the odds; the full count as a rite; a lost brother alive next door; the lost who weren't dead; a heroine who keeps the tally; a chalk-board ending; a Caraway episode title. Any one of these would pass. Together, the owner will read C as Caraway moved to Scotland.
   *Fix, most important first:*
   - **Replace the mint test.** Use a test that belongs to the place: the town's collies. Every ferry keeps a dog, and a dog's nose on the pier is how a pilgrim learns they are home. ("The dog knows before the lab does" is in the source, and Bran is already in the pitch.) The finale's household count then uses something other than mint.
   - **Drop the rule that mass overhead sets the odds.** Give the tide a different physical job. For example, the neighbours' moving sea shortens or steadies the allowance, so slack is when the frame holds longest. The cure can rest on C's own new rule (the lighter partner turns), which Caraway never had.
   - **Don't bring Callum back alive where the food fits.** Either he is alive next door and starving (their life has our hand after all), and the finale is the town learning to send food across by the handful: the town that fed its sons at the back door now feeds one it can never see. Or he is gone, and the gaining weight is something else the town has to work out.
   - **Rename** episode 8 and the Night of Leaves. Give Jess a job with its own texture (pier master, frame engineer) rather than keeper of the numbers.
2. **A shrine in disguise.** *Fix:* either make the Cure plainly commercial (a clinic and a hotel, paying patients, a price list, no saint), or show faith in a form nobody has seen. For example, the town's real devotion is to the printed Table itself: pinned by every door, quoted at weddings and in arguments, while nobody venerates a person. Cut the sleeve-touching and the "living saint".
3. **Ibsen's town meeting and the mayor from Jaws.** *Fix:* cut the church-hall meeting. Let the change spread without a speech. Duncan, the keeper of customs, runs his own jar in private because he doesn't believe Jess, and gets her answer. His family asks him, and the next household asks theirs. Show the town's refusal in the bookings for the Great Slack, not in a vote.
4. **The watch.** *Fix:* use a turned object that is neither a timepiece nor printed words. For example, his pocket corkscrew now spirals the wrong way, or a die from the pub's game has its faces running the wrong way round a corner. Let Del hand it over plainly rather than hide it in her leg.
5. **"Nobody remembers why."** *Fix:* cut the line. Let a ferryman give a confident wrong reason ("keeps the cold out"), so the audience can find the right one.

### Verdict

Not original enough to show the owner as it stands. Under the Scottish loch sit Caraway's mechanism, its taste test, its hand law, its lost-brother spine and its ending. Around them sit Lourdes and An Enemy of the People. Two ideas are worth keeping at any cost: the diving belt and the Strengthening. Built outward from those two, with a new test, a new job for the tide and a different fate for Callum, it could become a different and good show. As written, it is the pitch most likely to get "this is a rip of the last one".

---

## Taken together

A is the most original of the three and needs the least work. B has the best single invention (the bread) and needs three real repairs. C must be rebuilt around its two best ideas. Whichever goes forward should borrow A's refusal of the obvious ending: exile tried, and failing.
