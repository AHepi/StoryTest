## CONFIRMED findings

1. **Episode 6 — the ambush's seventh-night luck.** Six empty stakeout nights and a minute-resolution ticket carrying a 2.2-second hit are unexplained at the second reversal's hinge. (Ep 6)
2. **Episode 10 / master plan §8 — "three bodies" vs "two bodies."** The plan's canonical number is wrong and must be corrected to two (one nine times, one twice), as the episode has it. (Ep 10, plan §8)
3. **Episodes 1–10 — Liam's mother does not exist.** No trace of Danny's wife at the birthday, Rosie's collapse, or the minute beside Liam's reading, in a season that makes Aoife a person. (Eps 1–5, 10)
4. **Episodes 2 and 6 — Rosie's records-room casing is asserted, never shown.** A whole act turns on knowledge the audience never saw acquired, and the episode flags it ("You asked for the toilet." / "Course I did."). (Ep 2, Ep 6)
5. **Episodes 8 and 9 — Terry's stated refusal is reversed off-screen.** "Nobody's sending me in on my seconds, mind" against belted, nine legs, "Two's all I've ever asked for, love." (Ep 8, Ep 9)
