# 07 Owner feedback 1 - why Caraway was dropped

This is the biggest revision so far. It was made because of your feedback, not a critic's, so it is recorded here in full, like any other revision.

## What you said (26 September 2026)

> "Ok. Good start. But this is a direct rip of The Catch. And the religious angle is fine, but the way it is presented is unoriginal. Further, it shouldn't ever reference the semantics. There's a lot more to work with in Fourth Direction. Also directly naming popperian ideas is very on the nose. Try something other than Dystopia. Ok, go!!"

You also re-attached "The Fourth Direction". I checked the new file against the first one byte by byte, and they are identical.

## How I read it

Start the story over rather than patch Caraway. Six rules now apply to everything:

1. **Nothing from *The Catch*.**
2. **Religion only in a fresh form**, or not at all.
3. **No reference to the theory of explanation.** Not even as a book inside the story.
4. **Much more of *The Fourth Direction*.**
5. **No named ideas.** The message lives in what happens.
6. **Not a dystopia.**

The core message and the size (a full season) stay the same.

## What I found when I checked your point about *The Catch*

I had not seen *The Catch*. It is your own screenplay, set in the same Fourth Direction world, and your notes on it are on this repository's other branch (`26 Test - The Catch - two versions.md`). Reading them showed how close Caraway had come:

| In *The Catch* | In Caraway |
|---|---|
| A child called Nell | The heroine called Nell |
| The mint reveals who changed ("nothing has happened to the mint") | The mint test at the heart of the plot |
| Backwards signs and a backwards wristband seen through the heroine's eyes | The whole city's writing shown backwards, and the "Earth eyes" reveal |
| Turned germs that nothing will eat | The grey mould that nothing will eat |
| A heroine turned twice who can no longer share food with her husband and brother | Twins who end the season unable to eat the same food |
| A needle that kicks when something crosses | Needles as the main instrument of the plot |
| A kind figure from the neighbouring world who takes people | People sent to the neighbouring world, and a second society waiting there |

Several of these came straight from *The Fourth Direction*'s own ideas, which both stories drew on. But the choice of which ideas to build on, and how, was too close to yours.

The unoriginal religion: a found document copied as scripture, a high keeper, a heretic cell with a forbidden book, a rite at seventeen. All stock forms.

The references to the theory: the forbidden book in Caraway ("the Fable") was the theory itself, quoted aloud. Characters named after its symbols. A creed taken from it. The doom mechanism named its ideas in the plan.

## What changed

- **Caraway is dropped as the story.** Its files (01 to 06) stay in this folder as history. Nothing in them is deleted or rewritten.
- **The round 2 critique of Caraway was stopped** part-way when your feedback arrived. The three critiques were finished. Version 3 of its plan was written, but its revision log never was. Those files are kept and marked "stopped".
- **A new brief (08) went to the writers.** It carries:
  - your words;
  - the six rules;
  - a list of what *The Catch* and Caraway did, so neither is repeated;
  - a long inventory of *The Fourth Direction*'s unused material (heists, courts, surgery, spies, forgery, assassination by spin, first contact in mass, the mirror kitchen, and more);
  - a list of banned words.
- **The writers no longer receive the theory file at all.** It was moved out of their folder, so nothing from it can leak in.
- **Your craft skills are now used as guidance.** They are on the other branch: genre (with twelve genre profiles), plot, character, story-world and dialogue.
- **Each of the three new pitches starts from a different kind of story:**
  - a crime or gangster story of today;
  - a period piece set when the strange object first arrives, some time between 1880 and 1935;
  - a free choice that is neither of those.
- **Three independent reviewers** check the pitches before a plan is merged: one for originality, one for the message and your six rules, one for use of *The Fourth Direction* and how mind-bending it is.
- **The merged plan then gets two rounds of criticism**, and every change is logged, as before.

## What I'm unsure of

- I only know *The Catch* through your notes on it, not the screenplay. The new writers get the same notes, so an echo of something the notes don't mention could still slip through. If you spot one, tell me.
