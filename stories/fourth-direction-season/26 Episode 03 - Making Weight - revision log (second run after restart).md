# Episode 3, "Making Weight": revision log

The draft (`80-episode-03-draft.md`) is left as it was. The revised episode is `82-episode-03-final.md`. I checked every finding in the critique (`81-episode-03-critique.md`) against the master plan (`70-plan-v3.md`), against the draft, and against the finished episodes 1 and 2 (`82-episode-01-final.md`, `82-episode-02-final.md`).

**In short.** I accepted ten findings as they stood and three with a change (findings 1, 3 and 4). I rejected none outright. The critique made one factual slip, about when the tingle came in episode 1; its row explains it, and why that led me to a different number. Everything the critique asked me to keep is kept: the flash-forward cold open and its replay, Tess's night up and her walking in at the pot, Kieran under water as the clock in Ray's scene, "So someone's counting", the LM handprint, the thin lad at the bus stop and at the mews, Peg refusing to ring 999 with the tray on the floor, "And we clapped", Rosie's flowing mirror signature that Tess misses, and "Paper talks". I added no new rule and no new canonical number. None of the plan's banned words reaches the screen, and neither do "lift", "reserve", "mint" or a spoken "seconds" pun.

**A note on the file.** A file called `82-episode-03-final.md` already existed. It was made earlier in the night from a different critique of the same draft. This version replaces it and is built fresh from the draft and the current critique. I did read the log of that earlier version. Several of the extra changes at the end of this log were first raised there, and I checked each one against the plan and the finished episodes before making it.

## Other changes I made, and why

1. **Rosie no longer puts her hand inside Liam's handprint in the wall.** Her turned hand laid inside her brother's print is two hands meeting as mirror images across a barrier. That is The Catch's last image in another form, and the plan bans hands meeting on glass. Her glow now just slides over the print and the letters LM, going in and coming back. The print still does its job: Liam barrowed those beads, and he dies inside a bead floor in episode 5.

2. **Liam's line in the van.** The draft had Liam ask "Can she hear you?" But he was in the van at Mayfair when Danny said "She can't hear you, Tess" and Tess answered "I know." Now Liam says his dad's line himself, then asks what nobody asked in episode 1: "Then why d'you do it?" "So someone's counting."

3. **Peg's "Eats like a horse and never puts a gram on" became "Clears two plates and never puts a gram on."** In episodes 1 and 2 Rosie gives her plate away and is cheered for not eating; she has never been the family's big eater. The part of the line the audience re-reads later ("never puts a gram on") stays. It also avoids Peg saying "seconds" for a second helping, which would be the title pun again.

4. **"Two nights running", not three.** We only see Monday and Tuesday. Rosie now also tells the table what she saw on the Tuesday: "One old boy on nights, sits by the door, light out by two." That ties the torch in scene 2 to the sleeping guard in the black.

5. **Scene 2 picks up exactly where episode 2 ends:** Rosie walking after the grey van, saying its number plate over to herself.

6. **In the cold open and its replay, Rosie steadies herself with the flat of her hand, not her fingertips.** The show keeps close-ups of fingertips for the tingle that warns a crosser her time is running out. Fingertips on the dresser just before a crossing would muddle that signal.

7. **In scene 13 the office is empty when Rosie reaches for the pot.** The draft ended that moment with Terry "at the stove", but we never saw him come in, and she would never eat from his pot with him standing there. The moment now ends on the spoon standing in the pot.

8. **Lines no camera can film were cut or turned into something an actor can play.**
   - "She was fourteen that week. It was the week her own name went at the top of the board." Cut; episode 2 already gives Tess this in her own words, on the step.
   - "Tess has heard Terry say those exact words at the stove every morning for five years." Now she looks at the lard on Ray's knife and says nothing for a moment.
   - "Something Ray put in Tess's head goes quiet." Now she looks from Terry's tub to Rosie's empty plate, and picks up her fork.
   - "For the first time in her life, she doesn't know." Now: "Her lips have stopped. There's no number on them."
   - "She saw a knife spread that same white fat on a cracker two days ago." Cut; her stare does it.
   - "He counts it in his head, the weeks of fry-ups." Now Danny just puts his face in his hands.
   - Tess sitting up "as she has the night before every job since she was fourteen." Cut; Rosie's "Course you are" says it.

9. **The last scene is at half past five, not five, and it's still dark outside.** Sunrise that morning is after half past six, so the draft's "grey light at the blinds" couldn't be right. Episode 4 opens at 05:40 the same morning with the April dig, and the critique of episode 4 already assumes this episode ends at 5:30 with Danny reaching for his coat.

10. **Danny buckles on his rig's belt before he goes to post the tray into the safe.** Sending a hand through a steel door takes a rig, and in episodes 1 and 2 he always puts his on first.

11. **Peg hangs Rosie's coat on the hook when they come in,** so it's there for Tess to search. Afterwards Tess puts the leaflet back in the pocket, so Rosie still has it in episode 4, as the episode 4 draft expects.

12. **The notes at the end are rewritten to match,** with a short continuity note for whoever revises episode 4. This episode ends at 5:30 on Saturday 4 April. The writing test is now paid off here, on the back of the leaflet, so the episode 4 draft's letters in the windscreen fog would show it a second time. At the table Tess told the family about the two bodies and nothing else Ray said.
