## Episode 04

- I made the one change the plan lists for Episode 4, in scene 2, the war council at half past six on the Saturday morning. Tess's line about the grip now reads in full: "Collar does nothing. It's skin, or it's nothing. And don't think you'll do it with a posted hand. A hand still stood on the world can't hold anything that's off it. Slides off him like smoke." This is the ruling's fix for the hole where the audience could ask why Danny doesn't simply push a hand through a wall and grab Joe, the way he posts his hand into the safe later in the same episode. The line matches the show's already-declared rule that a sent hand stays fixed to the body on the leaf and can't grip anyone in the black, so it doesn't contradict episode 6, where Danny has to go into the black bodily to grab Joe's wrist. Rosie's line from the stairs about the hand being left behind follows unchanged, and so does everything else in the scene.
- Everything else in the episode is untouched: the cold open, the Post Office, the pit, the reefer raid, the Ahern scenes, the 02:04 ticket, the plants and payoffs section, and all the numbers, dates and names, so that none of the other episodes' set-ups or payoffs move.
- Planned changes I could not make: none. The plan lists exactly one change for Episode 4 and it is in. The other ten findings belong to episodes 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 and were left alone here on purpose, since changing them from this episode would break those episodes' own revisions.

## Episode 10

Changes made, all five of the plan's Episode 10 items:

1. **Scene 6, the investigators (noon).** Added the ruled line at the start of the investigator's second speech: "Your granddaughter walked off our Whitechapel ward at eleven that night, Mrs Marlow, and her next of kin is this address." with a beat before the existing "We'd like the family's weights, Mrs Marlow. To match." This gives the Register a stated reason to come to the weighbridge the morning after the raid, and it matches episode 9, where Tess says the ward rang her as next of kin the minute Rosie walked.

2. **Scene 7, the night of writing.** Inserted DANNY: "And I said yes. Write that first." immediately after Rosie's two lines about Liam and Con, with the existing "No reply. Kieran writes it, and the night goes on." now covering both confessions. This pays off Danny's "Did your sums do Liam too?" from episode 7 by having him own his "Yes" to Liam out loud, in the record, first.

3. **Scene 9, the baking tray.** Changed "the one that has lived there for twenty years and never come down for anyone" to "the one that has lived there for twenty years and come down once, for Rosie". This removes the small contradiction with episode 3, where Tess took the pencil down for Rosie's writing test, and it lines up with the matching fix made to episode 7.

4. **Scene 12, Rosie's return at the field.** Added her first words on landing, "Still me.", between "Wet grass, fog, the gate, her family." and Danny's "What did you see?". She settles her own toss at once, with no test, no print, no reading and no sign, which is the point of the line: the audience knows she can't prove it to anyone else, and the family doesn't ask her to.

5. **Scene 12, Terry's going.** Changed "nobody has brought one" to "nobody reaches for one". This kills the contradiction with Peg having brought her meter everywhere since episode 8 — the meter is in the house, not absent, and the choice made at four in the morning is not to use it, which is stronger.

Planned changes I could not make: none. All five items listed for Episode 10 in the revision plan were made exactly as ruled. No other line, scene, number or note was altered, so nothing in the other episodes breaks: the timings, weights and dialogue the surrounding episodes rely on are all unchanged.
