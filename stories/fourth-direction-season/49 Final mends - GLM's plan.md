### Episode 7
1. **Scene 9, the October '19 tide-book entry** (part of finding 4: the entry is the "six seconds all her life" claim in the book's own numbers, and it would contradict the corrected dialogue two lines below it).
   - OLD: "MAGGIE: October '19. The Mynors run. Six and a quarter." … "A tick above the 6."
   - NEW: "MAGGIE: October '19. The Mynors run. Four and a quarter." … "A tick above the 4."
2. **Scene 9, Maggie's history line.**
   - OLD: "MAGGIE: Aoife's, her dad on the wheel for her. That vault was her bread and butter. She'd walked it since she was fifteen — six seconds, in and out, all her life, tide or no tide."
   - NEW: "MAGGIE: Aoife's, her dad on the wheel for her. That vault was her bread and butter. She'd walked it since she was fifteen. Four seconds, when she started. Six, since the bank had the floor done in twenty-four — and she never asked why that was."
   - *Declared substitution:* the ruling's example reads "Six since the Marlows did the floor in twenty-four". "The Marlows" cannot be said here: scene 9 sits two scenes before the reveal that names them ("Yours, Tess. MARLOW on the side."), which is twist 8's plant, and the ruling's own first mend protects exactly that kind of early giveaway. The Marlows attribution lands in scene 11, unchanged. "Then nine" is likewise held for scene 11, which already establishes it ("A nine at nineteen, straight. The bank, October. Turned.").
3. **Scene 11, Tess's line** ("changes to match").
   - OLD: "TESS: That vault. She'd walked it since she was fifteen, you said. Every job in it six seconds, in and out, all her life, tide or no tide. Why does it come up nine in October?"
   - NEW: "TESS: That vault. She'd walked it since she was fifteen, you said. Four seconds when she started, and six since the floor went in. Why does it come up nine in October?"
   - Maggie's answer ("Because — …they'd had work done. There's a floor under the floor now…"), the flash, and "Yours, Tess. MARLOW on the side." are unchanged.
4. **Numbers used note** (the same finding echoed in the notes).
   - OLD: "the Mynors run, October '19, at six and a quarter (Aoife, straight)"
   - NEW: "the Mynors run, October '19, at four and a quarter (Aoife, straight)"

### Episode 10
1. **Scene 2, the loft: Frank's year moves from the roll to his kept tickets.** Replace the three paragraphs beginning "The job-day tickets run down them…" with:

   > The job-day lines run down them in purple ink gone lilac: a date, a time, a weight. No names. A roll never knew a name.
   >
   > Under the rolls, flat in the bottom of the box, lies an envelope gone the colour of tea. Nobody asks why these were never burned. Inside: Frank's tickets, torn off one by one, every one he ever printed, Peg's pencil still on them. Tess lays them along the boards in order, each against its date on the roll.
   >
   > The first: September, 78.0. On it, in pencil, in Peg's own hand from twenty-four years ago: *12 st 4*.
   >
   > Then week on week. 76.9. 74.4. 71.8. 69.0. 66.9. Kieran reads them out steady, like a lesson, because somebody has to. Nobody tells him to stop.
   >
   > The new year. 66.0. 65.9. 65.0. 64.8. Then spring, and the last of them: 64.4 — and on that one, in pencil, *10 st 2*, and under it, alone, *7.1*.

   And one line below them:
   - OLD: "The torch hums. Terry looks down the column the way only a man who has lived one can."
   - NEW: "The torch hums. Terry looks down the line of them the way only a man who has lived one can."

   Everything else in the scene stands: the unrolled rolls give the dates; Tess's "September. What was the job?" still works against the September ticket; scene 5's "Frank's last ticket. 64.4. *10 st 2.* *7.1.*" already reads correctly and needs no change.
2. **Scene 8, Terry's speech at the night of writing.**
   - OLD: "TERRY: Ta, Peg. *(beat)* Six goes to the end of my count, at my age, with the rigs gone to the police Sunday night. Or one go, and I land the right way round. Five year I've been the wrong hand at this table. Over there I'm not."
   - NEW: "TERRY: Ta, Peg. *(beat)* Six goes to the end of my count, at my age, and a warrant Monday. Or one go, and I land the right way round. Five year I've been the wrong hand at this table. Over there I'm not."
3. **Scene 12, the field: Terry's added line, before he goes.**
   - OLD: "TERRY: Aye."
   - NEW: "TERRY: Aye. *(beat)* And I'll not bite back. Whatever I land as over there, I stay."

   The action lines and the count ("One." … "Two.") are unchanged.
