## Format of the season
10 episodes of about 55 minutes, serialized drama. Each episode needs a title, a one-line pitch, a cold open, four or five acts, and an ending hook. At least one genuine twist per episode; three major reversals (around episodes 3-4, 6-7 and 10). Every twist must be planted fairly beforehand (list the plants). A twist earns its place only if swapping it for another would break earlier set-ups (hard-to-vary test).

## The theme must be carried by a mechanism, not a speech
The doom must follow from the culture not questioning, shown step by step, not from bad luck and not from one secret villain. Show the Semantics' pattern in human form: the culture's rules were fitted to past conditions; the world (often changed by the culture's own practices) produces a change outside that history; the culture can only answer with more of the same rules; that re-tuning makes it worse; only a new explanation, reached by someone daring to ask a new question, can repair it, and a single right person is not enough: the culture itself must become able to question. "Always dooms" must feel earned: the world always eventually changes outside what your rules were fitted to. Nobody lectures; characters discover.

## Seed concept (a strong starting point, not a cage; pitchers may keep, bend or replace any part)
WORLD. Ream, a walled city built around the Lift (the unpinning device), about 300 years after its founding. Its scripture, "the Leaves", is the Fourth Direction document recited as holy law: the seven laws, the theorems, and the "In the plot" examples, which are treated as prophecy and case law. The Established / Stipulated / Derived tags were lost in hand-copying, so a film's guesses and real physics sound equally holy. Liturgical fossils of the original phone page: a shoulder-tap blessing ("tap the stack at the top right"), the chant "two, three, five, seven", a Tally board where every crossing and turning is recorded but the "observed rate" line has been left blank for centuries, and the benediction "may the animations respect your reduced motion". Children are named after the Sources list (Abbott, Carroll, Wells, Plattner, Ehrenfest, Randall, Sundrum, Dvali, Adamala); offices too (the Randall is the high keeper; Accountants compute crossing budgets). A second, forbidden book, "the Fable" (the Semantics), is sealed as heresy; its secret readers, the Changers, take Greek-letter names (Theta, Tau, Lambda, Sigma, Pi) and have one creed: "What changes when things are changed?"
THE RITE. The Tithe: at seventeen every citizen crosses once (the original purpose, measuring the mirroring drift, is forgotten). Then the Mint Test: a leaf from the Sealed Jar. Caraway means Turned. The Turned are Passed, exiled through the Lift to "Minthome, where the mint tastes right", a joyful mercy. Holding hands in transit is forbidden because scripture says "One comes back mirrored. The handhold is what did it."
THE DOOM ENGINE (stipulated). Crossers whose time budget runs out become the Lost: clouds of nuclei hanging in the bulk beside the Lift, felt only as weight. Over 300 years, hundreds of tonnes. Their pull twists crossers: the drift is rising. The needles (gravimeters) register the growing "Weight of the Ancestors", which is celebrated as favour. More Turned, more Passings, more Lost, more drift. Each official response (fasting to lighten crossers, longer prayers, lighter children) is a re-tuning that makes it worse.
HEROINE. Nell Abbott, 17, apprentice Tallykeeper, good with numbers. Twin brother Wells. They hold hands in transit. Nell comes back Turned but says "mint" out of fear. Wells honestly tastes mint, but scripture says one hand-holder comes back mirrored, so the Randall must choose, and Wells is Passed. The family dog growls at Nell. Nell starves in secret (a Turned body lives only on water, spirit and tallow; mirror food is controlled by the Order's pantry); she is also immune to every disease, and her gut flora is mirror life to everyone around her. Want: prove Wells lives, bring him back. Flaw: the lie. Gift: she is the first in centuries to compute the observed rate.
OTHER SEED TWISTS. The needles' "Answering" of the prime chant is a real listener; Nell tests it by sending 11, an edit the liturgy forbids, and the answer is 13. The exile crossing is itself a coin toss, so the "cure" re-rolls the dice. The rule that exiled Wells was a screenplay beat. The relic "First Leaf" is a dead phone; powered, it shows the lost tags ("Stipulated: the film's own axiom") and a simulator whose drift slider a person set. Labels are relative: Ream may itself be "the neighbours" of the original document, and Minthome may be the old Earth. The Randall once questioned, knows more than she admits, chose certainty as mercy, and never computed the tally.
A META DEVICE WORTH KEEPING. In episode 1 the show teaches the audience the Leaves as if they were the show's own physics. Later the audience learns some were a film's guesses: the viewer made the culture's mistake.

## Craft rules from the owner's theories (apply them)
- Gap theory: manage the five gaps (world vs reality, breach, withholding, perspective, knowledge). Every open question is a mystery (promised an answer) or a wonder (promised to stay open); emphasis must match. The teller limits what can fairly be held back. The ending settles the gaps; theme is the story's answer to the question its world poses.
- Implied World: few departures with deep ripples; surplus detail taken for granted; a world with its own business; show the tip of the iceberg; the main departure should embody the theme.
- Anticipation: suspense = care x threat x uncertainty x time; climb the fear ladder slowly; show the rules, hide the thing; take away a refuge or make a necessity dangerous (eating!); break one genre contract early; choose who knows what, scene by scene; the ratchet; leave residue.
- Bond: care is a relationship built over time; alignment beats virtue; villains need care (the Randall must be understandable, even right about something); care is social; no fridging; surprise that stays in character deepens the bond.
- Hard to vary: every twist, scene and detail should do named jobs; if a detail can be swapped with nothing breaking, it is decoration, keep it only as deliberate surplus.

## Honesty ledger for the show itself
Like the Fourth Direction, the season must declare its own new stipulations once (e.g. that the Lost's weight raises the drift) in a short ledger in the season plan, and never add new ones in secret.
