# Round 2 critique: logic, second pass

Lens: contradictions introduced by the round 1 fixes, plus rulings on the "Still unresolved" items in `51-revision-log.md`. Round 1 findings that were fixed are not repeated. Arithmetic is shown where a number is at stake.

---

1. **The needle at the Graves reads "nothing" in episode 8, but the ledger puts Minthome's pile right under it**
   - **Where.** Ep 8 cold open ("at the river gate by the Old Graves, nothing... She stands at the Graves... where the needle is still"); twist table rows 8 ("the needle is still at the Graves") and 9 (plant: "the still needle at the Graves (8)"); 2.6 "Young and old clouds" and "Minthome's small weight"; ep 10 cold open (Theta's needle on the plinth since the night) and act 2 ("shows a small weight").
   - **Problem.** Round 1 fix L5 placed Minthome's 20 t pile a third as far from the Mother's door as Ream's cloud is from Ream's, so it pulls like about 540 t at Ream's distance. The Mother's door is the Graves door. Against the numbers table (Temple steps = 100 for 1,500 t), a needle on the old plinth must read about 100 × 540/1,500 ≈ **36**, a third of the Temple. It cannot be "nothing" in ep 8, and it is not "a small weight" in ep 10. Worse, in ep 10 Theta's needle has sat on the plinth since the night, so an instrument reading a third of the Temple's Weight sits beside Nell while she writes "2 IN 100". Either Theta hides it or Nell ignores it.
   - **Why it matters.** Major reversal 3 is meant to be fair and honest: the ledger card promises every later twist "can be worked out" (2.7). As written, what is shown on screen (a still needle at the Graves) contradicts the twist. The fix to make Nell's prediction fail honestly also makes her or Theta careless.
   - **Fix.**
     - Ep 8: the walk ends at the river gate, at least a hundred metres short of the old plinth. At that distance the pile gives about 0.05, which does read as "nothing". The gate is barred at night under the Pariah law, so the one spot she needed to measure is the one spot the old rules close. Change the line to "She stands at the barred river gate, looking down at the Graves". Twist table rows 8 and 9: "the needle is still at the river gate".
     - Ep 9 act 1: the "twenty-seven thousand times weaker" line stays, and it is a sum about Ream's Weight only. Moving the Great Tithe to dawn leaves no time to carry a needle to the plinth. That is honest, and it is the old rules' pressure at work.
     - Ep 10: Theta brings the needle down *after* the board is written (she spent dawn in the Temple watching the old needles for the Great Tithe bells), and sets it up while the cards run. Act 2: "Theta's needle on the plinth reads about a third of what the Temple needles read." Then WE. DIFFUSE. HERE. TOO. names it.

2. **"The mint alone is witness" breaks the no-secret-villain rule, and stipulation 7 now contradicts the reason given for it**
   - **Where.** 1.8 reason 4 ("A hidden Turned Randall carved 'The mint alone is witness' long ago. It closes every test a liar could not pass: a signature, a sheet of paper, a dog"); 1.3; 1.4; 2.4; twist table ep 9 ("the two tests her office's gloss forbade: a hand and a leaf").
   - **Problem.**
     - **Ruling: yes, it breaks the rule.** A gloss secretly invented by one office-holder to protect her own kind is a conspiracy. It sits at the season's first pivot (Wells's exile) and holds up the silence (reason 4). The season's own case against conspiracy (Theta is wrong, "the villain was love", the Year 235 relabel is sincere and public, "There is no villain in this chain") then has one real conspiracy in it.
     - There is also a new contradiction. Round 1 fix L2 (stipulation 7) makes each separate thing take its own chance. So a sheet of paper carried through the Lift says nothing about whether the body turned, and paper is *not* a test "a liar could not pass". The Leaves' corollary "Paper is a witness... tells the police whether the thief turned" is therefore false in this world, and 2.4 does not list it.
   - **Why it matters.** The theme needs the doom and the silence to come from rules fitted to past cases, not from one person's cover-up. This one is cheap to repair, and the repair uses the new stipulation.
   - **Fix.**
     - Make the gloss sincere, public and fitted to a real failure. In about Year 190 the Order tested Tithe children by the paper slips sewn in their hems, and legal papers go in pairs ("paper is a witness"). One child came back with her two slips disagreeing, one reversed and one not, and she tasted mint. The Randall of the day carved "The mint alone is witness" in daylight. She was right about paper. Later readers took the gloss to close the signature and the dog as well, though neither had ever failed. Hidden Turned Randalls kept it gladly, which is silence out of self-interest, not invention. This is the Semantics' pattern: a rule selected by one past failure, stretched past the history it was fitted to.
     - Rewrite 1.8 reason 4: "It closes the two tests a liar could not pass: a signature and a dog."
     - Add to 2.4: "'Paper is a witness' (of a crosser). Here a carried sheet takes its own chance, so one sheet witnesses only itself; many sheets witness the rate."
     - Keep the kennel placement accidental: the kennels have stood by the Randall's kitchen since the founders, for the scraps.
     - Optional echo in ep 7: the clipped card pairs that come back mismatched are the Year 190 event repeated. Pim: "That's why the gloss."
     - Twist table ep 9: "confesses by the test her office's gloss forbade (a hand) and the one it kept (a leaf)".

3. **Mira cannot be closed "on her count" and still be in a pair, and the pair rule removes her secret fast's effect**
   - **Where.** Ep 1 cold open ("Ten. Fourteen. Sixteen." "The Sundrum closes the field on her count"); 1.2 and the change log ("a pair is closed on the heavier crosser's count"); 1.4 ("They go in pairs"); supporting cast (Mira: "the Fast before the Fast"); twist table ep 5 ("Mira, the lightest girl in her year, was the Fast's first victim"); 2.6 "The Fast kills".
   - **Problem.** Mira is the lightest girl in her year, so any partner is heavier, and the field closes on the partner's shorter count, not her 16 seconds. Under the round 1 pair rule, her secret fast bought her no extra time in the dark, so it could not have killed her.
   - **Why it matters.** Mira is the Fast's first victim and the ep 5 twist's emotional proof. As written, the ledger says her fast did nothing.
   - **Fix.** One clause in the ep 1 cold open: Mira is the day's odd child and crosses alone. That is also a stronger image. Add to 2.6 "The Fast kills": "In a pair the heavier crosser's weight sets the count, so one faster in a pair gains no time. The Lightening works on everyone because everyone fasts. A lone crosser's fast counts in full."

4. **"About 7 in 100" and "eight tonnes a year" need a population history, and constant tonnage contradicts 12,000 people**
   - **Where.** 1.9 (Years 131–235, "about eight tonnes a year"); step 3 ("eight tonnes a year for 170 years"); ep 2 act 3; numbers table; 2.6 "Young and old clouds" ("Ream's own Weight around Year 200").
   - **Problem.**
     - With a steady population, the stated curve (2 until Year 150, then 3 / 5 / 10 / 24) averages about **4.2 in 100** over three centuries, not 7.
     - A steady eight tonnes a year means about 130 funerals a year, a town of about 5,000–6,500, every year from 131 to 300. Twelve thousand people in Year 300 bury about 15 t a year.
   - **Why it matters.** "Everyone is comfortable" with 7 in ep 2 is the hinge of Nell's "each year?" question. The numbers must survive a viewer with a calculator, and they feed the Minthome comparison.
   - **Fix.** State the population history once in 1.9 and the numbers table: **120 founders; about 3,000 by Year 131 (a fast-growing first century); 12,000 by Year 300 (slow, steady growth).** Weighted by Tithe cohorts, the grand total comes to 7.0 in 100.
     - Funerals then add about 4 t a year in 131, rising to about 15 t now. The Weight comes to about 250 t by Year 180, 400 t by 200, 540 t around **Year 220**, about 700 t at capture (235), about 1,250 t by 283, and about 1,500 t by 300 once the plague year's dead, the Called, the Joined and the lost Passed are counted.
     - Replace "about eight tonnes a year" (1.9, step 3) with "from about four tonnes a year then to about fifteen now".
     - In 2.6, change "Ream's own Weight around Year 200" to "around Year 220, when its rate was about 4 in 100". The Year 220 rate from the curve is 4.4, so ep 10's 41 in 1,000 still fits.
     - Add one line: capture came at about 700 t, so Minthome's 540-t-equivalent pile is below it, which is why Wells can still follow the far pull out of the Mother.
     - "Stopped at three hundred tonnes" now falls about Year 190, when the rate (about 3.4) was already visible to anyone counting. It still holds.

5. **The ballast message cannot be read, and the bier's weight is left out of the sum**
   - **Where.** Ep 9 act 3 ("THE DOOR. MOVE. Then the ballast"); Minthome thread ("At twenty... the count stops"); ep 10 act 3 ("lies on the Givings bier... sits up among the graves, a dead man on a bier"); numbers table (Wells).
   - **Problem.**
     - After two words, a bare run of hauls is just a number. Nothing tells Kit it means kilograms of stone for Wells.
     - Hester sends a stone amount (26) worked from a guess at his weight (44 kg) made across two worlds. Kit, who has scales, needs the *target*.
     - If the bier crosses, as "sits up... on a bier" says, its weight joins the load. His count is then shorter than the 70 kg that Hester chose as safe, and that risks the very falling-short the ballast was meant to avoid.
   - **Why it matters.** The finale's uncertainty rests on this message. As written, an attentive viewer can see that Kit could not decode it.
   - **Fix.**
     - Send words the code can carry, from the verse every child recites in ep 1's cold open ("...still registers **seventy kilograms**"): SEVENTY. KILOGRAMS. Adamala's people break in during the counts for KILOGRAMS, so Wells receives SEVENTY and a broken word. He finishes the verse himself and chooses. The Giving's own verse brings a boy home.
     - Anchor Hester's 70 on screen: "He made it at seventy. Nothing heavier is proven." (Wells arrived at 70.4 kg.)
     - The bier: either it stays on the Mother's ring floor and he crosses in his white wrapping, or, better, Wells weighs the bier in with his stone. That is why "he does not say how much stone he has chosen", and it is one more choice of his own.
     - Update the numbers table accordingly.

6. **Ruling on the three "asserted" physics beats: two follow from the ledger as it stands; one needs stipulation 3 reworded, with no new stipulation**
   - **Where.** 2.5 stipulation 3; 2.6 "Arrival and loss on the needle", "A hand, not a body", "Reaching the other world takes time"; ep 6 act 1; ep 9 act 3; ep 10 act 3.
   - **(a) Theta's needle rises, holds and falls as Wells arrives *into* Ream. It follows.**
     - The ledger lines are Departure 3 (a needle feels a body in the dark beside a door), "Doors... line up" (the dark beside the Mother *is* the dark beside the Graves) and "Arrival and loss on the needle" (leaving the dark alive = a rise that falls).
     - The limit: a fall shows only that he left the dark alive, on *some* world. Because a Givings field sets nothing down, he cannot be put back on the Mother's ring, but he could still have stepped out on Earth.
     - Keep "Arrived", which is exactly the founders' meaning of rise-and-fall, and keep the city's cheer until he sits up, as staged. Say in 2.6 that the rise sits on top of the Mother pile's steady reading (see #1).
   - **(b) A hand is set down inside rock. It follows.** The parents are stipulation 6 (the ring sets down what is within reach), 2.6 "a crossing is a slide in the fourth direction, which keeps every other coordinate", and rule 5 (an unpinned hand passes through stone). It is set down where it is, and where it is is inside stone. Rewrite the 2.6 line to cite all three. No declaration is needed.
   - **(c) A count that is too short "falls short of the world". This does not follow as written, and it is load-bearing** (the upper limit on the ballast).
     - Stipulation 3 says whoever follows a pull out of the ring's reach "and still has time left steps out onto that world". That can be read as "out of reach with any time left means you step out", and on that reading no one ever falls short.
     - Reword stipulation 3 before the ep 6 card airs. This clarifies a declared line and adds no new one: "...steps out onto that world if their time lasts the journey across; a crosser whose time runs out on the way stays in the dark."
     - Give no number. The only use (Hester's 70) is anchored in evidence, see #5.
     - Log the rewording in the revision log.

7. **The hand's count in episode 6 contradicts the 700 ÷ kilograms rule**
   - **Where.** Ep 6 act 1 ("tingling fingertips... the real collapse a heartbeat after she pulls free"; the relic "in the dark... for most of a minute"); 1.2; 2.6 ("a hand's count runs for minutes").
   - **Problem.** Owen sets the field on Nell's arm alone. An arm of about 2.5 kg plus the relic gives about 700 ÷ 2.7 ≈ 260 seconds, over four minutes. The scene ends with fingertips tingling and the field collapsing after about one minute.
   - **Why it matters.** Episode 6 is where the ledger card airs. Its own set piece should not break the working number shown on it.
   - **Fix.**
     - End the ratchet on the *closing* danger, not the running-out danger. The Warden's steps force Owen to close, and a hand still in the stone would be set down in it. Owen closes a heartbeat after she pulls free. Cut "tingling" and "collapse".
     - Keep "most of a minute". By the crates (30 kg ≈ 23 s: nearly 2 in 5; 5 kg ≈ 140 s: 62 in 100), a minute beside the Weight gives the relic roughly even odds, so "It did not turn" is a real coin toss.
     - If the count itself must be the threat, the relic's time rises to four minutes and it becomes more likely than not to turn. Do not do that.

   - **Where.** Section 7 rows "The Randall feeds Nell" (T/T ✔ ep5), "The Weight sits at the door" (Ran ✔ ep8, Pim ✔ ep8), "Wells offered himself" (City ✔ ep9); mysteries table ("Why can Nell, and we, read the plates? raised 2 (Nell asks)"); ep 8 act 2 (Theta tells Dimopoulos "that the Randall has been shielding her").

     - Move City to ✔ ep10 for Wells's offer (he "tells them where he has been").

9. **The Weight-book cannot both keep "ARRIVED" for two centuries and have its old rise-and-fall labels scraped to "REFUSED"**
   - **Where.** Ep 7 act 4; 1.9 Year 235; Theta's wound.
   - **Problem.** If the Year 235 ceremony re-inked the old rise-and-fall entries, Nell could not read two centuries of "ARRIVED". If it did not, nothing old became "REFUSED", and the text does not say what *was* scraped, or which change of words Theta saw at thirteen.
   - **Why it matters.** This is a prop Nell reads on camera, and it carries Theta's "they changed the words, not the needle".
   - **Fix.** Say it exactly. Pre-235 rise-and-fall entries keep ARRIVED. What the ceremony scraped was the handful of rise-and-stay Passings from the years just before it, first written LOST, and re-inked RECEIVED. From the ceremony on, the clerks write RECEIVED and REFUSED. Theta saw LOST become RECEIVED over the same needle traces.

10. **The clock and the rates are slightly off in four places**
    - **Where.** 5 "The season clock"; 10 rule 1 and ep 3 act 4; ep 2 act 3; ep 10 act 1 and the numbers table.
    - **Problem and fix.**
      - The clock says "then seven days (episodes 9–10)". The beats run about three days: ep 8 night → Day 1 (Pim, the gloss) → Night 1 (the Randall chalks 24) → Day 2 (the board, the Tithe moved to dawn) → Night 2 (the carrying) → Day 3 (ep 10). Write "then three days, inside Wells's seven". This also explains why Wells is alive but on a bier.
      - "A minute a haul" and "89 hauls take a night" do not agree, since 89 hauls at that pace is an hour and a half. Write "a minute a haul, with rests; 89 hauls take half the night and every hand".
      - "Year-end sums" in ep 2 fall just after the spring Tithe. Either say Ream's year ends with the Tithe, or call them "the Tithe sums".
      - "31 in 100" for 50-kg crates at the old door does not fit ep 5's own crates. At 70 kg ≈ 10 s it is 1 in 5, and at 30 kg ≈ 23 s nearly 2 in 5, so 50 kg ≈ 14 s gives about 25. The Weight grew by under 1% from spring to autumn (13 Joined, a season of funerals, the crush dead), which at the steep post-capture slope adds under a point. Use **26 in 100** ("Twenty-six down to four"), or raise ep 5's 70-kg figure to "1 in 4".
