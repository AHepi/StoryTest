The ruling's own edit to the master plan (declaring the tingle's two-second lead in the budget paragraph and the ledger row) is already applied and touches no episode. Every change below is a line, a clause, or one short exchange. No new scenes.

## Part 2 — episode by episode

### Episode 1

1. **Scene 7 (the pit).** After TESS: "Worse. Your ears'll swear you're falling. You're not. There's nowhere to fall.", insert:

   KIERAN: What if you pressed it in here?
   TESS: Then you'd be carrying the pit with you. Water counts. Everything counts.

   Then "When can I go in?" continues as written. Nothing else in the episode changes.
2. **Plants and payoffs (consequential, finding 14).** The bullet "**The pit (9).**" gains the new exchange and its job: why a field fired in water cannot carry her, paid in ep 9 when her thumb stops an inch off the wrist unit.

### Episode 2

1. **Scene 6.** Danny's line becomes:

   DANNY: Banks. Banks I've never heard of. And Mynors had us back last autumn, for the floor under the one we did in '24. Two floors now. Long time to be nowhere. *(the phone buzzes again; he waves it quiet)* One wants Marlow Mix in a basement that hasn't got a vault in it yet.

   Peg's "Three times what we had on Monday." follows as written. **Scene 5 (the bank on Cheapside) is untouched.**
2. **Plants and payoffs (consequential, finding 5).** The bullet "Danny's unnamed City vault" becomes: Danny's City vault — Mynors, named at the table in scene 6 (so Tess hears it), and told again unnamed at the bank in scene 5. The rest of the bullet stands.

### Episode 7

1. **Scene 1 (cold open).** A caption over the opening shot: **TWO WEEKS LATER.**
2. **Scene 3.** The note reads: FERRY. NOBODY LOW. THURSDAY 21ST. JUST YOU.
3. **Scene 4 (the loft).** Lee's column, keeping every beat of the surrounding dialogue:

   TESS: Lee. November, sixty-four. December, fifty-nine. Fifty-seven. Fifty-five. January, fifty-three. February, fifty-two. *(beat)* Canary Wharf, March. April: fifty-six. May: sixty.

   "Nine and a half. *(beat)* Same as Mayfair, near enough." stands (52 kg buys 9.6 seconds by the painted rule).
4. **Scene 6.** After "Tess turns it off. She isn't listening anyway.", insert: TESS: *(to the windscreen)* Two weeks I've had that in my pocket. Then her lips move as written.
5. **Scene 9.** In the counting rhythm, before Terry's eleven ticks, insert:

   MAGGIE: October '19. The Mynors run. Six and a quarter.
   TESS: Whose?
   MAGGIE: Aoife's, her dad on the wheel for her. That vault was her bread and butter. She'd walked it since she was fifteen — six seconds, in and out, all her life, tide or no tide.

   A tick above the 6. Scene 11's "She'd walked it since she was fifteen, you said…" then stands unchanged.
6. **Scene 11.** The flashback becomes the table, not the bank yard:

   **Flash — the weighbridge office, a Tuesday evening in March.** Tea, the order book, Danny's phone buzzing on the table. DANNY: *"And Mynors had us back last autumn, for the floor under the one we did in '24. Two floors now. Long time to be nowhere."* And he drinks his tea, and Peg counts the pages of the order book, and Tess, at the table, takes it for a man counting work, because they all did.

   The words match episode 2's new line. Maggie's placing of the bank ("Mynors. Cheapside. Two doors down from the one that flooded") is unchanged.
7. **Scene 12.** Rosie's confession: "At Mayfair. There was never anybody there. Their lad was six metres off with a stopwatch, timing me, and I never knew him from a pull in my gut." becomes "At Mayfair. There was never anybody near me. I never felt a thing." The rest of the confession stands.
8. **Scene 12.** Tess's cure line: "We've got rigs. We can cure them here. Rosie. Terry. Danny. Tonight if we—" becomes "We've got rigs. Rosie. Tonight. Terry at five, and again, and again. Danny—" *(She stops.)* The sentence about the book before it, and Rosie's "She's right, Nan." after it, stand.
9. **Numbers used (consequential, finding 8).** "Lee's curve, 81 down to 66 and back" becomes "Lee's curve, 64 down to 52 and back, his Canary Wharf at nine and a half". No other episode carries Lee's weights.
10. **Plants and payoffs (consequential, findings 5 and 9).** The Danny's-boast bullet now cites the table telling in scene 6 and says the flash is the March table at Grays as it happened. The binder bullet's quotation "We can cure them here" becomes "Rosie tonight, Terry at five and again".
