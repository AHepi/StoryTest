## Final mends (5, each a line or less)

1. **Episode 10, scene 8: Terry's line gives away the family's vote before there is a vote.** Fable 1, and my own round-5 wording caused it: "…with the rigs gone to the police Sunday night." The vote is not put until scene 13 and settled in scene 14. It becomes "Six goes to the end of my count, at my age, and a warrant Monday. Or one go, and I land the right way round."
2. **Episode 10: Terry could bite home cured, so the finale's stated stakes are false.** This is the checker's one confirmed finding. Episode 9 teaches that a turned crosser who turns on the way back comes home straight, and Rosie bites home from the same shore minutes before. Terry adds one line before he goes, for example: "And I'll not bite back. Whatever I land as over there, I stay."
3. **Episode 10, scene 2: Frank's name and Peg's pencil sit on the audit roll, though a roll is the printer's own copy.** Fable 2. Episodes 1 and 7 show that a roll gives a date and a weight only; names and pencil belong on torn-off tickets, which Marlow tickets are burned. Fable's better beat is to find Frank's actual tickets in the 02 box, under the roll, kept in an envelope and never burned, with Peg's pencil on them ("12 st 4" and the rest). Tess reads them off the tickets, not the roll. Keep everything else in the scene.
4. **Episode 7: Aoife's bank was "six seconds … all her life", but Danny's crew poured its first floor in 2024.** Fable 3; my own round-1 ruling had GLM add that history. Maggie's line becomes, for example: "Four seconds, when she started. Six since the Marlows did the floor in twenty-four, and she never asked why that was. Then nine." Any line of Tess's that repeats "six seconds … all her life" changes to match.
5. **Episode 4, scene 9: Con places Aoife's death on "the pavement, with the buses going by"; episode 7 places it off the Ahern pontoon, at the lock-up wall.** Fable 4. Con's line becomes "We stood our minute on our own quay, with the tide going out under us."

**Also correct (one wrong word), episode 8:** "It turns east along the river road, and Tess follows it out of Tilbury, through Purfleet". Purfleet is west of Tilbury, so "turns east" becomes "turns west".
