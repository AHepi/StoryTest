## Fix (8 findings, each a line or a short exchange)

1. **Episode 10, scene 8: Terry chooses exile over the cheaper cure he reads aloud, and nobody weighs the two.** Fable 1.
   - After he reads his odds, add one short exchange that sets the two routes side by side in the family's own words. For example:
     - Tess: "Five seconds, six goes. Two in three you're eating here by Monday."
     - Terry: "Six goes to the end of my count, at my age, with the rigs gone to the police Sunday night. Or one go, and I land the right way round. Five year I've been the wrong hand at this table. Over there I'm not."
   - Let Peg's "Terry. Turned. Twenty twenty-one" come just before his answer, so he goes even acknowledged.
   - In scene 10, after Ray's "About four in ten", add one line, for example: "And if it don't, we go again before the warrant."
2. **Episodes 4 to 7: who sent the bullet is never said.** Fable 2. The plan counts sending Joe to Peg's safe among Maggie's secrets, so Maggie owns it. On the ferry, answering Tess's "And the boats? The bullet?", Maggie adds one line before "Nobody's square", for example: "Mine. I sent a bullet to a boy's bed and rang for pills at breakfast. I've not squared that either."
3. **Episodes 6 and 9: Danny's "early tingle" proves nothing.** Fable 3. I overruled this in round 1; Fable now shows it is a false clue.
   - At 2.4 seconds the tingle comes at 0.4, and at 2.2 at 0.2. Either way it comes before he could reach Joe, and after the six empty nights he would know it always does.
   - Cut "Tingle came early. That's the grab." (and the "fizzing before I ever touched him" line beside it) in episode 6.
   - Cut "I was fizzing before I ever touched that lad." in episode 9.
   - Keep "The bridge has had me at two point four since April." / "Two point two."
   - Tess's line in episode 6, "It comes two seconds before you're done", may stay if it still does work in the scene; otherwise cut it with the rest.
4. **Episode 7, scene 5: Kieran knows the Nobody low "to the minute", though episode 1 shows the tables can't.** Fable 4, and its round-1 quibble 7.
   - Cut Tess's "When's Nobody low tonight?" and Kieran's tide answer, down to "That's the bit Miss likes."
   - Open the exchange on the ticket: "I want your minus-fifty-nine. Just for tonight."
   - In episode 10, scene 9, Kieran reads the time off Maggie's book, which lies open in front of him: "Maggie's book says five fifty-two. Two hours."
5. **Episode 8, scene 7: Peg knows the Army's delivery day with no source.** Fable 5; my own round-3 fix added the line. Give it to the hatch, where lorry drivers eat every morning. Earlier in the scene, Mick says, for example: "Purfleet's shut to us Wednesday fortnight, Peg. Army's in. Whole shed."
6. **Episodes 2 and 6: Rosie's casing of the clinic's records room is asserted, never shown.** MiMo 4; the continuity critic raised it in round 1. In episode 2, at the clinic, add one wordless-to-brief beat: Rosie asks the receptionist "Where's your toilet?", and on the way down the corridor she passes a steel door marked RECORDS. STAFF ONLY, with a keypad. Two seconds.
7. **Episodes 8 and 9: Terry says at the vote "Nobody's sending me in on my seconds, mind", then crosses nine times.** MiMo 5. In episode 9, before his first leg, Terry adds one line that owns the change, for example: "Two seconds I can do. It was the long ones I said no to."
8. **The whole season: Rosie and Liam's mother never exists.** MiMo 3; the people critic raised it in round 2.
   - Add one wordless beat, where it fits best (at the birthday in episode 5, or at the minute beside Liam's reading): a framed photograph of a young woman on the office wall beside Frank's, and Danny's glance at it.
   - If a line is wanted, one only, in the family's manner, for example Peg: "Their mother's been in Grays churchyard since Liam was four. This house doesn't say her name."
   - No more than that.
