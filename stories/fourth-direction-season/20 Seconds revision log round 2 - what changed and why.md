# SECONDS: revision log, round 2 (plan version 2 to version 3)

**What this is.** Three critics read plan version 2 (`50-plan-v2.md`). One watched it as an ordinary viewer would, episode by episode (`60-critique-r2-viewer.md`). One re-checked every law, number and rule (`60-critique-r2-laws.md`). One tested every fix made in round 1 to see whether it had broken something else (`60-critique-r2-patches.md`). I checked every finding against the plan, the brief, your second feedback and The Fourth Direction itself, and against The Catch notes and pitches B and C where a finding said something echoed them. Version 3 is `70-plan-v3.md`. Version 2 is kept unchanged.

**Verdicts used.** *Accepted:* the problem is real and the fix went in. *Accepted in part:* the problem is real, but part of the fix was changed or left out, for the reason given. *Rejected:* the finding is wrong, or its fix would make the plan worse. No finding was rejected outright this round. Several critics found the same problem, and where two of them suggested different fixes I chose one and say why.

**The short version.** The critics caught three kinds of trouble. First, a handful of things still looked like The Catch (a live reading climbing in a dark room, a reserve light on the rig, a marmalade taste test, "the papers are printed funny", and a story about deciding for other people) or like pitches B and C (a jar of the neighbours' sea tested against a turned person, someone refusing to hold the old man's hand at the fence, the flu). All of these are gone or changed. Second, some of the physics pointed the wrong way: taking the top off a distant hill would make Grays scales read heavy, not light; a body in the black over a scale pulls it up, not down; Liam's sum showed the scale killing him on its own. All fixed, and the fixes made the story better. Third, the viewer found the first two reversals were guessable an episode early; both now have a real turn for the audience.

## 4. Changes made to keep the plan coherent

Nobody asked for these directly; each was needed so the accepted fixes fit together.

- **Still six guesses, and no seventh.** Guesses 4 (where the April change happens), 5 (the bite) and 6 (the grip) were revised in place and each says so. New lines in the "derived" list: the field's own glow is the only light; the ripple (forced by the book's own examples); the minus reading; more than one tug besides home; a new tug takes longer to find. One line moved out of "derived" into the world's history (how dangerous mirror germs are).
- **Where things are in the office.** Peg's safe now stands at the end of the little bridge's plate, and at night the scales' displays are dark and they only print. That makes the minus ticket possible and keeps every unseen visitor off a live screen.
- **Episode 2, act three rebuilt.** With the flu and the passport gone, it now holds Kieran's cold and Terry's soup, Rosie showing him surgeon videos, Terry's call with Con, and the unplaced Ahern steelyard.
- **Episode 10 re-ordered.** Act one: Frank, then the investigators. Act two: the night of confessing, the die, the baking tray. Act three: 05:52. Act four: what the rolls prove, and Danny's stove. Act five: the vote.
- **Rosie next door.** In the sea (episode 9) she holds her one breath of their air until she lands; the screen stays black; she drops eight metres home. On the shore (episode 10) she stands a minute before the bite, screen black, and keeps her last breath to herself.
- **The Trading Standards letter** now says the change is "beneath the estuary", to match the dig.
