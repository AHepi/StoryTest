## CONFIRMED findings

1. **Weigh-wall range contradicts the set pieces it must serve** — the four-metre limit (Ray, ep 3) is broken by Corbett's fifty-foot flood and by the 3:12 log of a body at three fathoms; the plan's rule 13 carries the same error. *(Eps 3, 7, 9, 10; plan §3.2/§13)*
2. **Maggie's printout is a live reading of an unseen body** — "It's still printing" breaches plan §14's ban and edges toward The Catch's needle. *(Ep 5)*
3. **Ray's decisive knowledge and his case transfer off-screen** — the spinner, the drift, and the 59.0 match are never delivered to him; the planted notebook page never fires; his ep 10 request precedes any count. *(Eps 5, 6, 8, 10)*
4. **Rosie's lie is never weighed against its cost** — the confession prices shame and applause, not the war it launched or Con, whom the raid killed. *(Eps 3, 4, 5, 7, 10)*
5. **Kieran's log is internally incoherent** — months of "Same as every day" against "The tide's in my graph", and an unexplained wobble on the device that proves Liam's death. *(Eps 2, 4, 6, 7, 9)*
6. **"Two bodies" at Purfleet vs. the plan's exact "three"** — the episode is right and the master plan's number is wrong; the finale's evidence chain depends on it. *(Ep 10; plan §8, §14)*
7. **"I heard it land" breaks the black's silence** — sound does not cross; the crossing grammar everywhere else is heartbeat and breath. *(Ep 6)*
8. **The ferry graph marks "straight" and "no change" with the same tick** — and its own line ("never a tick the other way") collides with the ticks it draws; this is the episode's thesis object. *(Ep 7)*
9. **Peg and Tess never speak after the exile** — the season's deepest care-debt, raised by the Frank revelation, is left entirely unaddressed in acts or words through the epilogue. *(Eps 7–10, epilogue)*
