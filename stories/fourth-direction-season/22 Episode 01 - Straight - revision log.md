# Episode 1, "Straight": revision log

The draft (`80-episode-01-draft.md`) is left as it was. The revised episode is `82-episode-01-final.md`. I checked every finding in the critique (`81-episode-01-critique.md`) against the master plan (`70-plan-v3.md`) and the draft before deciding. All eleven things the critique asked me to protect are kept word for word. No new rule, number or name from the plan's banned list was added.

## Other changes I made, and why

- **The crisp packet.** In the draft Rosie grinned "too quick" and then didn't take a crisp, which stacked two more hints on top of the plan's one (the long look). Now she grins and takes one. The long look is still there for sharp viewers. This goes with finding 1.
- **How the canvas travels.** Rosie now unwraps it from its tea towel at the Ferry House and wraps it back in the same towel. Small, but it means the tea towel is in the right place at every step, down to the one Tess drops over it when Ray arrives.
- **The plants list at the end** has been rewritten to match. Rosie's removed hints are gone from it. New entries cover the tingle's timing, the double life at the hatch, Tess's "Near enough isn't" and "First time, every time", the MR F. MARLOW envelope, Peg's meter, the box of weights, Terry's new song, and how the surgery works.
- **Length.** The episode is about 800 words longer, mostly the fuller plants list, the hatch beat and Kieran's push in Terry's scene. The screen time per act is about the same.
