## Episode 07

**Changes made (all four ordered for this episode):**

1. **Scene 9, the October '19 tide-book entry.** Maggie's read-out for the Mynors run changed from "Six and a quarter" to "Four and a quarter", and the mark it gets changed from "A tick above the 6" to "A tick above the 4". Done because the finding said the vault's history couldn't have been six seconds back in 2019, since the first Marlow floor wasn't poured until 2024.

2. **Scene 9, Maggie's history of Aoife's vault.** Her line "She'd walked it since she was fifteen — six seconds, in and out, all her life, tide or no tide" became "She'd walked it since she was fifteen. Four seconds, when she started. Six, since the bank had the floor done in twenty-four — and she never asked why that was." I used the ruling's wording with the one declared substitution the plan allows: where the ruling's example said "the Marlows did the floor in twenty-four", I wrote "the bank had the floor done", because this scene sits two scenes before the reveal that names the Marlows ("Yours, Tess. MARLOW on the side."), and naming them here would give away twist 8's plant — the exact kind of early giveaway the ruling's first mend protects against.

3. **Scene 11, Tess's "Why nine?" speech.** Her line "Every job in it six seconds, in and out, all her life, tide or no tide" became "Four seconds when she started, and six since the floor went in." This repeats the corrected history so the two women's exchange matches the new numbers. Maggie's answer, the flashback and the Marlows reveal are untouched.

4. **Numbers used note.** "the Mynors run, October '19, at six and a quarter (Aoife, straight)" became "at four and a quarter (Aoife, straight)", so the episode's own record matches the corrected dialogue.

**Planned changes I could not make:** none. All four changes listed for Episode 7 were made.

**One thing noticed and left alone, per the plan's scope rule.** Tess's speech to the family in scene 12 still says "It was six seconds, Danny, all her life", which echoes the old history. The plan orders changes only in scenes 9 and 11 plus the numbers note, and says nothing else changes, so I left it exactly as it was. Flagging it here in case the owner wants it added to a later round.

## Episode 10

1. **Scene 2 (the loft): Frank's falling year now comes off his kept tickets, not the printed roll.** I replaced the three old paragraphs that put "FRANK 78.0" and Peg's pencil marks directly on the audit roll with the plan's new text: the roll now shows only date, time and weight ("No names. A roll never knew a name."), and an envelope of Frank's own torn-off tickets, with Peg's pencil still on them, is found in the bottom of the 02 box and laid against the roll's dates. The reason for the change: a roll is the printer's own copy and never carried a name, and the pencil notes (12 st 4, 10 st 2, 7.1) belonged on the torn-off tickets, which is also how episode 1 set up tickets being kept and burned. "Beside it" and "beside that one" became "On it" and "on that one" to match the tickets.

2. **Scene 2, one line below the new paragraphs:** "Terry looks down the column" became "Terry looks down the line of them", because Frank's year is now a line of separate tickets on the boards rather than a column printed on a roll.

Before, Terry was speaking as if the family had already decided to hand everything over, hours before the vote in act five; now he only names the deadline everyone already knows from the investigators' visit.

The action lines and his count ("One." … "Two.") are unchanged, as the plan ordered.

5. **Everything else was left exactly as it was**, including scene 5's "Frank's last ticket. 64.4. *10 st 2.* *7.1.*", which already reads correctly with the ticket change, and the episode's plants and payoffs notes, whose line about "Frank's falling tickets with Peg's own pencil on them" also already matches.
