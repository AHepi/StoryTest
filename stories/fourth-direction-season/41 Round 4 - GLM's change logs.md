## Episode 02

Changes made to this episode:

1. **The planned change, made in full.** In the Tuesday-morning scene where Peg explains her plan (Act one, Scene 4), I added two lines exactly where the revision plan says: after Peg finishes explaining that they'll hop the painting loose until it comes back the right way, Liam asks "Won't she come back with it? Nothing else in the plants section was touched: the existing continuity note ("nobody in the family states the cargo rule before 3") still holds, since Peg's answer is a dismissal of the rule, not a statement of it.

No planned changes for Episode 2 were left out. The plan listed only the Scene 4 insertion for this episode, and it is in, word for word, in the right place. ## Episode 06

Changes made to this episode, both in the cold open:

1. **The six nights before, explained.** Right after the line "Seventh night. If the tow runs, the man comes again.", I added the two sentences the plan asked for: "Six nights he has stepped in at four past by the clock and found the black empty. Six crossings, two seconds each, off the plate, and nobody told." This says plainly what the "seventh night" only implied: Danny has been going in blind at four past two every night for a week, spending two seconds a go, standing off the plate so nothing prints, and telling no one. It also squares with the rest of the episode: it explains why the boy's ticket is the only one ("Just the one on it"), why the earlier nights left no −59.0 tickets (nobody came, and Danny stood off the plate), and why Tess says "You went alone."

2. **The envelope catch, made honest with the clocks.** I changed "a white envelope lies on the flat of the painting, let go a moment ago" to "a white envelope lies on the flat of the painting, and the fingers that let it go are still sliding back out of the steel." The intruder is now caught in the middle of the drop, hand still withdrawing through the safe door, rather than after it. This widens the timing window honestly: a boy weighing fifty-nine kilos has over eight seconds to spend, so his hand can still be coming out while Danny's two-plus seconds are nearly spent; under the old wording Danny would have had to arrive after the drop was finished, which squeezes both crossings tighter than the numbers allow. As the plan ordered, the body stays unnamed here, and Joe is still identified only in Scene 2.

No planned change for this episode was left out; the plan listed exactly these two, and both are in. Everything else — every scene, line, number, and the plants and payoffs and numbers sections at the end — is word for word as it was, since the plan asked for nothing further and I did not want to risk knocking anything against the other episodes. The six two-second crossings added in change 1 are background only; nothing in this or any later episode depends on their count, so I did not add them to the "Numbers used" list, which the plan left untouched.

## Episode 10

Changes made to Episode 10:

1. In Act three, Scene 12 ("The black — Ray"), I removed the words "the table's grain, the flue's old soot, and" from the description of what Ray's glow shows him in the black. Before, his glow seemed to light the whole office, which breaks the rule that the glow only reaches about an arm's length.
