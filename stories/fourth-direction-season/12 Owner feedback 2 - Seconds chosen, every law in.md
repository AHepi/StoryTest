## The checklist: every item must be in the plan, true in the world, and doing a job

**Law 1, the stack.**
- Reality is a stack of three-dimensional leaves along a fourth direction, a hair's breadth apart.
- Matter, light and every force but gravity stay on their leaf.
- The space between is black: you cannot look at a neighbour, only weigh them.
- *Stipulated:* there are many leaves, they carry worlds like ours, and things can leave them.

**Law 2, unpinning.**
- A field frees matter from its leaf for a bounded time.
- Off the leaf, atoms have no stable shape (pull thins with the cube of distance, so orbits and atoms fail).
- *Stipulated:* the field holds atoms together while it lasts, and lasts longer the less mass it carries.
- So crossings are short, timed and limited by mass.
- Everything inside the bubble crosses too: the air in your lungs, the food in your stomach.
- Whatever is still off the leaf when the time ends stays there as a spreading cloud with only gravity left to feel.
- Also: fingertips tingle as time runs out; a partial return leaves a clean flat plane, bloodless for a second; send a hand, not a body; an amputee gains extra seconds.

**Law 3, translation is free.**
- A move along the fourth direction passes over every wall without touching it.
- Every knot comes undone.
- An unpinned eye sees the inside of everything within its bubble, lit only by the light inside the bubble; beyond that, black.

**Law 4, rotation mirrors.**
- A turn through a plane containing the fourth direction brings a body back as its mirror image, down to every molecule.
- A slide never does.

**Law 5, no up in the dark.**
- There is no floor and no orbit, so nothing tells a crosser which way they face.
- A turn and a slide feel identical from inside.
- Every crossing is a coin toss that cannot be felt or watched, fixed at the instant the time runs out.
- The drift (the real chance of turning) need not be one half, and only counting many crossings reveals it.
- The only cue is the tug of home. Follow the wrong tug and you step out one leaf over.
- Two crossers holding on to each other are turned relative to each other by the grip.

**Law 6, handedness is fate.**
- A mirrored body runs on mirrored chemistry. Ordinary food and drugs do not fit it, and ordinary germs cannot infect it.
- Its own germs become mirror life, which nothing on this world can recognise, digest or fight.
- A turned person lives on water, spirits and fat.
- Mirror food is costly to make.
- A dog knows the smell.
- Looking-glass milk cannot feed a child born on this leaf.
- Because eyes and memory turn too, no looking can settle who flipped. Only chemistry against a sample that never crossed can.

**Law 7, neighbours weigh.**
- Gravity alone crosses between leaves, and across the gap it fades with the cube of distance.
- A neighbour is felt as mass and nothing else.
- *Stipulated:* strongly enough to feel at human scale.
- The only channel between leaves is mass in motion: slow, heavy, and impossible to fake.

**The stipulation for the ending, now true: the neighbouring leaf's life is the other hand.** For a turned person, the world next door is where the food fits.

**The seven theorems, all true and all used:**
1. Walls become weights: security is redesigned around mass and time.
2. Privacy has a radius: to see inside you, the watcher must be beside you, and so can be weighed.
3. The cure uses the same dice: the only cure for a turned crosser is another crossing with the same odds and risk, so the drift is the most valuable secret on Earth.
4. The mirror is relative: whoever holds the reference sample decides who counts as normal, and sealed jars can be lifted.
5. Custody by pantry: the state cannot hold a turned person, only feed them.
6. One honest channel: messages in mass are slow and cannot be forged.
7. Exile is a cure: for a turned crosser, home is one leaf over.

**The corollaries:**
- paper is a witness;
- passports record handedness;
- the lightest courier goes furthest;
- a crosser carries their own air (the only sample of the neighbours' air is a held breath);
- weightlessness is the crossers' school;
- murder by mirror takes six months and looks like a diet;
- the dog knows before the lab does;
- astronomy becomes neighbourhood watch.

**Every "In the plot" example** from the source should appear somewhere if it fits SECONDS, each doing work. Each one left out needs a reason, given in the law ledger.
