# 37 Round 2 - GLM's change logs, episode by episode

*GLM's own account of each change it made to carry out my round-2 ruling (file 36). Episodes 1, 2, 4, 8 and 9 needed no changes.*

## Episode 03

- Changed one thing, in scene 5 (the pit): Ray's line about the Mayfair wall's log now reads "Every body in the black within ten metres" instead of "within four metres". This is fix 1 in the revision plan, bringing the episode in line with the master plan's range so it no longer contradicts Joe being six metres off at Mayfair without being logged as a different body.
- I made no other changes. The plan says for this episode "Nothing else in the episode or its notes; no range is quoted there", so I left the plants and payoffs, the numbers note, and every other scene exactly as they were, since no other range is quoted anywhere in the episode and the other eight fixes belong to episodes 5, 6, 7 and 10.
- There were no planned changes for Episode 3 that I could not make.

## Episode 05

- In Scene 16 (the lock-up), I changed Maggie's line "It's still printing." to "It printed all night." This is the only change the plan lists for Episode 5 (fix 2). The reason for the change is that watching a live reading of an unseen visitor broke the show's rule that no instrument ever shows an unseen visitor live; the reading had to be found on paper instead. Everything around the line — the stage direction showing she has torn the curl of thermal paper off her own kitchen printer and brought it out, and her next line, "He's on my wall." — already matched the fix, so nothing else needed touching.
- I made no other changes anywhere in the episode, including the Plants and payoffs section, because the plan lists only one item for Episode 5, and the notes never quote the old "still printing" line, so they needed no correction to stay consistent.
- There were no planned changes for Episode 5 that I could not make; the one planned change was made in full, word for word as the plan gives it.

## Episode 06

- **Cold open, scene 1 (fix 7).** I added a short line for Danny, spoken under his breath, right after "The scale's display is dark. There is nothing in this room to watch but the clock." and before the tow's horn. It explains why he ambushes blind instead of waiting for the printer: the plate only prints after a body settles, so by then the visitor would be gone, and he has to already be in the black. I used the plan's wording exactly. Everything before and after that point is untouched.
- **Scene 4, the Ahern kitchen (fix 3).** I changed Joe's answer about the letter from "Went in. I heard it land." to "Went in. I let it go in the box." The reason is the finding itself: sound cannot cross through the black, so Joe could not have heard the letter land from in there; saying he released it inside the safe keeps his meaning without breaking the rule. The *(beat)* and "Then he took hold." are unchanged.
- **Scene 4, the Ahern kitchen (fix 8).** I replaced Maggie's standalone line "Thursday fortnight, then." with her longer speech: "I rang that house once and got a dial tone. Post a letter there and it goes in a drawer. A thing in that safe gets read. *(beat)* Thursday fortnight, then." This explains why she sends the ferry invitation into the black by her son rather than phoning or posting it. The plan's wording is kept exactly, the tide-book beat before it is unchanged, and Joe's next lines follow as before.
- **Plants and payoffs notes (fix 3).** I updated the one place in the notes that quoted Joe's old line, so it now reads "Went in. I let it go in the box". The plan ordered nothing else changed in the notes, so I changed nothing else there — in particular I did not add new entries describing the Danny or Maggie lines, and the note "said alone under the clock" still stands, since Danny is still alone when he mutters it.

All four changes the plan lists for Episode 6 have been made, in the plan's own words where the plan gave wording. There were no planned changes I could not make. I made no other changes, so nothing here breaks the other episodes: Maggie still ends on "Thursday fortnight, then.", which episode 7's note ("Maggie's 'Thursday fortnight' sets it") depends on, and the envelope, its wording and everything downstream of it are untouched.

## Episode 07

Changes made, all from the revision plan's Episode 7 list:

1. **Scene 4 (the loft) — Lee's weight readings changed (fix 6).** Tess now reads Lee's column as: November sixty-seven, December sixty-two, sixty, fifty-eight, January fifty-six, February fifty-five, then after the March job fifty-nine and sixty-three. His lowest weight, fifty-five kilos, now buys exactly nine seconds under the family's painted rule, so the job no longer beats "the longest job a Marlow's ever done".

2. **Scene 4 — narration changed (fix 6).** "Maureen's six and a half, Lee's nine and a half" is now "Lee's nine".

3. **Scene 4 — Terry's age question changed (fix 6).** "You were, what, sixteen?" is now "twenty-one?", because Lee's Canary Wharf job was in 2009 and Tess is thirty-eight in 2026.

4. **Scene 4 — Tess's job length changed (fix 6).** "Nine and a half. Same as Mayfair, near enough." is now "Nine. Same as Aoife's, near enough." I kept the plan's exact wording even though it means Tess names Aoife's nine at midday, hours before Maggie supplies it on the ferry that night; the plan notes this itself and orders nothing beyond the ruled line, so I added nothing to explain it.

5. **Scene 9 (the ferry graph) — Terry's hops are now noughts, not ticks (fix 4).** "eleven small ticks in a row" became "eleven small noughts in a row"; Maggie's line became "Eleven goes and never a mark either way"; and "the fence of ticks" became "the fence of noughts". A tick on the sheet now only ever means a crossing that came back straight. The later phrase "the fence of eleven above the 2" (scene 12) was left exactly as the plan says.

6. **Scene 11 — the timetable now travels with Tess (fix 5).** Maggie now pushes the folded sheet across the table to Tess ("Yours. I've the books.") and puts only the tide book in her bag, instead of tucking the sheet inside the tide book. The timetable is no longer in two places at once, and her later "My half's in here" now plainly means the tide books.

7. **Scene 11 — Tess's exit changed (fix 5).** She now walks off the pontoon "with the ticket in her coat and the folded timetable inside it", so her taking it out of her coat in scene 12 follows properly.

8. **Scene 11 — the mended entry changed (fix 6).** "right as rain after Canary Wharf. Nine and a half." is now "Nine."

9. **Notes, Plants and payoffs (fix 4).** Terry's eleven hops are now described as "plotted as eleven noughts and no cross and no cure".

10. **Notes, Numbers used (fix 6).** "Lee's curve, 64 down to 52 and back, his Canary Wharf at nine and a half" is now "67 down to 55 and back, his Canary Wharf at nine".

Everything else in the episode is untouched. One small thing I noticed and left alone on purpose: in scene 12 Peg still burns "the ticks and the crosses and the two circles" — that stays right, because ticks still mark the crossings that came back straight; only Terry's eleven hops changed their mark. All the planned changes for Episode 7 were made; none had to be left out.
