## Episode 01

Changes made to this episode, following the revision plan:

1. **Scene 7 (the pit) — the planned insert.** After Tess's "Worse. Your ears'll swear you're falling. You're not. There's nowhere to fall." and before Kieran's "When can I go in?", I added the two-line exchange the plan orders: Kieran asks "What if you pressed it in here?" and Tess answers "Then you'd be carrying the pit with you. Water counts. Everything counts." This is finding 14: from now on the audience knows a field fired in water would carry the water with it, so when Rosie's thumb stops an inch off the wrist unit in the Deep in episode 9, the moment is paid for and needs no new rule. I kept the lines exactly as the plan gives them and put them where the plan says, so "When can I go in?" still follows as written.

2. **Plants and payoffs — the "The pit (9)" bullet.** As the plan's consequential item for finding 14, I added the new exchange to that bullet and said what it sets up: why a field fired in water cannot carry her, paid in episode 9 when Rosie's thumb stops an inch off the wrist unit. The rest of the bullet (the goggles, the bubbles line, "There's nowhere to fall") is unchanged.

Planned changes I could not make: none. The plan lists exactly two items for Episode 1 (the scene 7 insert and the plants-and-payoffs bullet), and both are in. I deliberately touched nothing else — no other lines, scenes, numbers or plants — because every other finding in the plan belongs to later episodes, and changing shared material here (Danny's vault dialogue, the note's date, Lee's weights, and so on) would break the fixes those episodes are waiting to receive.

## Episode 07

Changes made, in plain words:

1. **The two-week gap is now marked (the plan's finding about the unmarked gap).** A caption reading "TWO WEEKS LATER" was added over the first shot of the cold open, Maggie's note now reads "FERRY. NOBODY LOW. THURSDAY 21ST. JUST YOU." instead of leaving the Thursday undated, and Tess got one new line in the car on the way to the ferry — "Two weeks I've had that in my pocket." — right after she switches the radio off, so we see she has carried the envelope unopened since episode 6. Everything around those additions is untouched.

2. **Lee's weights were lowered (the plan's finding that his old weights could not buy nine and a half seconds).** In the loft scene his column now runs 64 in November, down through 59, 57, 55, 53 to 52 by February, then 56 in April and 60 in May after Canary Wharf. The shape of the curve, Terry's "Right as rain after Canary Wharf", and every other beat of the scene are unchanged. The numbers list at the end was updated to match ("64 down to 52 and back").

3. **Maggie now gives the Mynors history herself (the plan's finding that Tess quoted history Maggie never gave).** During the counting on the ferry, before Terry's eleven ticks, Maggie reads out "October '19. The Mynors run. Six and a quarter," says it was Aoife's with her dad on the wheel, and says the vault was her bread and butter, walked since she was fifteen, six seconds in and out all her life. A tick goes above the 6. That makes Tess's later "She'd walked it since she was fifteen, you said" something Maggie has actually said on screen.

4. **The flashback is now the table, not the bank yard (the plan's finding about the boast Tess never heard).** In the Mynors reveal, the flash of Danny at the Cheapside bank was replaced with a flash of Danny saying it at the family table one Tuesday evening in March, using word for word the line the plan assigns to episode 2's scene 6 ("And Mynors had us back last autumn, for the floor under the one we did in '24. Two floors now. Long time to be nowhere."), with Tess present in the room. Maggie's placing of the bank ("Mynors. Cheapside. Two doors down from the one that flooded") and Tess's "He told us... 5. **Rosie's confession no longer leaks ferry-only knowledge.** She now says "At Mayfair. There was never anybody near me. The rest of the confession, including the shame and "I'm going first", stands as it was. Maggie's own telling on the ferry ("three fathoms", the stopwatch) is untouched, since it is hers to tell.

6. **Tess no longer offers a ten-second cure to men who cannot buy ten seconds.** Her line at the table is now "We've got rigs. Rosie. Tonight. Terry at five, and again, and again. Danny—" and she stops, because Terry's weight buys about five seconds and Danny's buys nothing. The sentence about the clinic's book before it, and Rosie's "She's right, Nan." after it, are unchanged.

7. **The notes at the end were updated to match.** The drift-table bullet's quotation changed from "We can cure them here" to the new cure line; the Danny's-boast bullet now cites the table telling in episode 2's scene 6 and says the flash is the March table at Grays as it happened; the envelope bullet notes the two weeks and the dated note; and the numbers list gained two consequential entries — the Mynors run of October '19 at six and a quarter, and the note's date of Thursday the 21st — both now spoken or shown on screen and so both needing to be logged.

Every change the plan lists for Episode 7 has been made. None was impossible, and nothing else in the episode was altered, so no other episode's set-ups are affected: Lee's weights appear in no other episode, the flashback now matches episode 2's revised line exactly, and the scene 11 dialogue the insertion supports ("you said") needed no edit once Maggie had said it.
