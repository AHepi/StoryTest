# Episode 1, "Straight": revision log

The draft (`80-episode-01-draft.md`) is left exactly as it was. The revised episode is `82-episode-01-final.md`.

I checked every finding in the critique (`81-episode-01-critique.md`) against the master plan (`70-plan-v3.md`) and against the draft itself before deciding anything. An earlier pass of this revision was already in the folder, and the finished episodes 2 and 3 were written to fit it. I didn't take that pass on trust. I went through every finding again from scratch, and I agreed with nearly every decision in it, so I kept those, which also means episodes 2 and 3 still fit. I then made a few more small fixes of my own. They are listed under "Other changes".

All eleven things the critique asked me to protect are still there, word for word. No new rule, no new number and none of the plan's banned words has been added.

## Other changes I made, and why

- **The crisp packet.** In the draft Rosie grinned "too quick" and then didn't take a crisp. That stacked two extra hints on top of the plan's one, the long look at the packet. Now she grins and takes one. The long look is still there for sharp viewers. This goes with finding 1.
- **Her route through the bank, re-timed (new in this pass).** The earlier revision had her reach the crate at 2.7 and then take more than four seconds to come back. That doesn't add up, and it made Tess's 9.4 look padded. Now going in and coming back take about the same time, the wall floods while she's still on her way, and she wins the canvas from the beads by half a second, as the plan describes. No later episode depends on these tenths.
- **"She turns with the canvas" is now "She swings round with the canvas" (new).** In this show "turn" means coming back mirror-turned. The old wording, at the very moment the weight passes, could suggest she turned right then, or that the passing weight did it. In fact the coin toss is fixed when her time runs out.
- **Where the catalogue picture comes from (new).** The draft had Peg cut it out of "the morning paper", but no morning paper could carry a theft done at three that morning. Now she has the news up on the office computer, prints the page, and trims the picture square with the kitchen scissors, headline (STOLEN FROM MAYFAIR BANK) and all. It is still the "catalogue cutting" that later episodes talk about, so nothing else has to change.
- **"Tickets don't leave this room" is now "a Marlow's ticket never leaves this room" (new).** Mick's ticket has just gone out through the hatch, so the old wording wasn't true.
- **Where the canvas is at every moment.** Peg folds a clean tea towel round the painting when Danny takes it out of the safe on Saturday. Rosie unwraps it at the Ferry House and wraps it back up, and Tess drops a tea towel over it when Ray arrives. Small, but the canvas is now always where the next scene needs it.
- **Scene count.** Nineteen scenes become eighteen, because scenes 12 and 13 were merged (finding 12).
- **The plants list at the end** is rewritten to match. The Rosie hints I removed are gone from it. New entries cover the tingle and her route, the double life at the hatch, Tess's "Near enough isn't" and "First time, every time", the MR F. MARLOW envelope, Peg's meter, the box of weights, Terry's new song, where the catalogue picture comes from, and how the surgery works.
- **Length.** The episode is about 950 words longer than the draft, mostly the fuller plants list, the hatch beat and Kieran's push in Terry's scene. Screen time per act is about the same.

## Notes for other writers and for the plan's keeper

- **The tingle, before episode 6 is written.** Episode 1 shows only that the tingle comes near the end of Rosie's seconds. The finished episode 3 and the episode 5 draft have settled on "about two seconds before you're done", and in the episode 5 draft Rosie says so out loud. That works for Rosie and Liam. But Danny has only 2.2 real seconds in episode 6. A fixed two-second warning would start about a fifth of a second after he steps in, before he even finds Joe, so he couldn't blame it on the grab ("Tingle came early. That's the grab."). The plan doesn't say how the warning works. Before episode 6 is written, the keeper should decide between a fixed time before the end and a share of whatever seconds you have. Until then it would be safer if Rosie's line in episode 5 said "near the end" rather than a number of seconds.
- **Peg's ordinary glucose reading.** It has no number in the plan, so it appears as "an ordinary morning number". If one is wanted on screen, it belongs in section 14.
