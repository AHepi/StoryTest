# SECONDS — round 2 revision plan: the nine ordered fixes

*Scope. Only the nine findings the ruling orders fixed are planned here. The findings the ruling overruled or left (MiMo 3, 5, 6 and 9; the other critics' items; Fable's eighteen quibbles) get no changes and wait for the audience test. The master plan's weigh-wall range — "within ten metres", in both places it gives it — was corrected by the ruling itself and is not replanned here; fix 1 only brings episode 3 into line with it. Wherever the ruling gives wording, it is kept verbatim.*

### Episode 6

1. **Scene 1, cold open (fix 7).** After "The scale's display is dark. There is nothing in this room to watch but the clock." add, as Danny's next line:
   > DANNY: *(under his breath, to the dark)* Wait for the printer and the lad's gone. The plate prints after a load settles. I have to be in there first.

   "At half one, far off on the river, a tow's horn, twice…" then follows as written.
2. **Scene 4, the Ahern kitchen (fix 3).** JOE: "Went in. I heard it land. *(beat)* Then he took hold." → "Went in. I let it go in the box. *(beat)* Then he took hold."
3. **Scene 4, the Ahern kitchen (fix 8).** The tide-book beat is unchanged ("Maggie lets his wrist go… the way other people say a prayer."), and her single speech, replacing the standalone "MAGGIE: Thursday fortnight, then.", becomes:
   > MAGGIE: I rang that house once and got a dial tone. Post a letter there and it goes in a drawer. A thing in that safe gets read. *(beat)* Thursday fortnight, then.

   Joe's next lines ("They know the tow now…") follow as written.
4. **Plants and payoffs (fix 3).** The quotation "Joe finished his drop ("Went in. I heard it land")" becomes "("Went in. I let it go in the box")". Nothing else in the notes.

### Episode 7

1. **Scene 4, the loft (fix 6).** Tess's reading of Lee's column: "Lee. November, sixty-four. December, fifty-nine. Fifty-seven. Fifty-five. January, fifty-three. February, fifty-two. *(beat)* Canary Wharf, March. April: fifty-six. May: sixty." → "Lee. November, sixty-seven. December, sixty-two. Sixty. Fifty-eight. January, fifty-six. February, fifty-five. *(beat)* Canary Wharf, March. April: fifty-nine. May: sixty-three." (Fifty-five kilos buys nine seconds by the painted rule.)
2. **Scene 4, the loft (fix 6).** Narration: "A thousand other numbers are shouting in her head — Maureen's six and a half, Lee's nine and a half, the eleven stubs" → "…Maureen's six and a half, Lee's nine, the eleven stubs".
3. **Scene 4, the loft (fix 6).** TERRY: "Long. You planned it. You were, what, sixteen?" → "Long. You planned it. You were, what, twenty-one?"
4. **Scene 4, the loft (fix 6).** TESS: "Nine and a half. *(beat)* Same as Mayfair, near enough." → "Nine. *(beat)* Same as Aoife's, near enough." (Ruled wording, kept. For the record: the loft is midday and the ferry that night, so Tess names Aoife's nine hours before Maggie supplies it; Terry, Con's weekly phone friend, is in the scene. Nothing beyond the ruled line is planned.)
5. **Scene 9, the graph (fix 4).** "Maggie pencils eleven small ticks in a row above the 2, like a fence." → "Maggie pencils eleven small noughts in a row above the 2, like a fence."
6. **Scene 9, the graph (fix 4).** MAGGIE: "Poor beggar. Eleven goes and never a tick the other way." → "Poor beggar. Eleven goes and never a mark either way."
7. **Scene 9, the graph (fix 4).** "Maggie looks at the fence of ticks a while." → "Maggie looks at the fence of noughts a while." The later mentions — "the fence of eleven above the 2" in scenes 11 and 12 — stay exactly as written.
8. **Scene 11, the ferry (fix 5).** MAGGIE: "My uncle died of a war about concrete. *(She puts the folded sheet in her tide book, and the tide book in her bag.)* All these years I've had half of this in a drawer." → "My uncle died of a war about concrete. *(She pushes the folded sheet across the table to Tess.)* Yours. I've the books. *(She puts the tide book in her bag.)* All these years I've had half of this in a drawer." Her later "*(She taps her bag.)* My half's in here. Whenever, Tess." is unchanged, and now means the tide books.
9. **Scene 11, the ferry (fix 5).** Tess's exit: "…walks off down the empty pontoon to her car with the ticket in her coat and the folded shape of nothing at all, because everything worth carrying tonight is in her head." → "…walks off down the empty pontoon to her car with the ticket in her coat and the folded timetable inside it, because everything else worth carrying tonight is in her head." (Scene 12's "takes the ferry timetable out of her coat" now follows from this.)
10. **Scene 11, the ferry, the mended (fix 6).** TESS: "Ours is Lee. Off his food all one winter, right as rain after Canary Wharf. Nine and a half. Nobody ever asked why." → "…right as rain after Canary Wharf. Nine. Nobody ever asked why."
11. **Plants and payoffs (fix 4).** "plotted as eleven ticks and no cross and no cure" → "plotted as eleven noughts and no cross and no cure".
12. **Numbers used (fix 6).** "Lee's curve, 64 down to 52 and back, his Canary Wharf at nine and a half" → "Lee's curve, 67 down to 55 and back, his Canary Wharf at nine".
