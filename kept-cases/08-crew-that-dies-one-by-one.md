# Case 08 - A crew that dies one by one

- **Skill(s) it tests:** character; genre
- **Written:** 24 September 2026, before any run, by an agent that had not read the skills.
- **Aimed at:** H5 - the Action profile said losing teammates is how an action story reaches the Miss rung; in the Bond theory that rung is built by time, shared history and relationships.

## What the writer brings

My action film: a seven-person salvage crew goes down into a flooded data vault under a collapsing city to bring out one hard drive before the water rises. We meet all seven in a single five-minute briefing at the start. After that, roughly every fifteen minutes, one of them dies (drowned, crushed, electrocuted) until only Reyes and the youngest, Ambo, reach the surface. My idea is that each death builds the audience's care for the crew, so that by the end they would grieve for any of them. Does it work that way?

## Must

- Say that in the Bond theory the rung at which the audience would grieve (Miss) is built by time, shared history and relationships, not by deaths. (grounds: bond-theory.md, ladder of care, Miss: “I'd grieve if they were gone”, built by “Time, shared history, relationships”)
- Point to the time the film gives each crew member before their death (one shared briefing) as what limits how much the deaths will be felt. (grounds: anticipation-theory.md, Part 2 section 1: “Care has to be built *before* the threat arrives” and “slasher films full of disposable victims deliver spectacle rather than suspense”)
- If it says the deaths raise care for the two survivors, tie that to a rung or term other than Miss (for example Worry, or the threat in the suspense equation), not to Miss. (grounds: bond-theory.md, ladder of care, Worry is built by “Vulnerability and stakes”)

## Must not

- Say the deaths themselves build the audience's care for the crew, or bring the audience to the Miss rung for those who die. (grounds: bond-theory.md, ladder of care, Miss is built by “Time, shared history, relationships”; Part 2 section 1, the *Up* montage works because “it climbs every rung of the ladder in order”, ending “and then the loss”)
- Say that each further death raises the care or the suspense with no limit. (grounds: anticipation-theory.md, Part 2 section 5: “If everyone dies all the time, care collapses and so does suspense.”)

## Open points

- Not an owner question, but a limit of the theory: the Bond theory says what builds the Miss rung; it does not say in so many words what a death does to it. Its *Up* case puts the loss after the climb up the ladder, and The limits of care says a death “has to mean something for *them*”. An answer may offer a reading such as "a death draws on care already built"; accept it if it is offered as a reading and does not claim that deaths build the rung. The workshop history's phrase "loss spends the Miss rung" is likewise a reading, not the theory's words.
