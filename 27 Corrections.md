# 27 Corrections

The workshop's record of its own errors: the kinds seen so far and what catches each now, every full correction (open and closed), and, at the end, the errors made before this file existed. How to use it: the `error-correction` skill, and its `references/recording.md`. Anything that waits on the owner lives in `22 Questions - meanings only you can settle.md`; a correction here only points to it.

## Kinds of error seen here

When a new error is of a kind listed here and got past the catch named for it, the catch itself becomes the target of a correction. *By itself* means a script or hook runs it every time; *on trust* means an agent must follow a step, and the trace it leaves is named.
